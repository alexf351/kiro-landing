About Iro AI.

Iro AI is a game that teaches anyone to use AI effectively in their daily life, 5 minutes a day. Most AI learning is a 20-minute YouTube video, a saved prompt pack, or a course you quit halfway through. Iro turns AI into short daily reps instead: writing tighter prompts, improving weak outputs, spotting hallucinations, and building workflows you'll actually reuse. Made for people who don't know where to start. Real tasks, not party tricks.

What Iro AI is

Iro AI is a mobile learning app for practical AI skills. It teaches users how to work with modern AI tools through short lessons, exercises, Prompt Lab practice, timed duels, weekly AI news, XP, streaks, and ranks.

The app is built for people who want to understand and use AI more effectively without committing to a long course upfront.

What Iro covers

  • ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and AI tools.
  • Prompt engineering and AI foundations.
  • AI agents, automation, AI coding, and vibe coding.
  • Creative workflows including image and video generation.
  • Work and career use cases including business, marketing, finance, managers, healthcare, and job hunting.

Who's behind Iro AI

Iro AI was founded by Alex Furukawa. Alex works in private equity real estate, where he leads his firm's AI initiative and has built a series of automation tools his team uses on live deals. Iro grew out of that work. The fastest way to get good at AI is to point it at something real, not to watch a course about it. At Iro he leads product and writing, and focuses on practical AI fluency: prompting, choosing and using tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity, spotting AI mistakes, and the daily habits that turn AI from a novelty into hours you get back. He shares what he learns on the Iro AI blog, LinkedIn, and X.

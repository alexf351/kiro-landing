The best AI apps for iPhone, in short

For most people, the best AI apps for iPhone are ChatGPT as the everyday assistant and Perplexity for research, plus a specialist only if there's a job you do every week.

Swap ChatGPT for Claude if you write long documents, for Gemini if you live in Gmail and Google Docs, or for Copilot if your day runs on Word, Excel and Teams. Everything below is free to download from the App Store, and most of these apps charge for heavier use.

Best AI assistant apps for iPhone: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and Grok

  • ChatGPT: the default. The app covers voice conversations, questions about photos you snap, and image generation, and it's the assistant Apple connects to Siri. The catch: on the free plan, uploads, image creation and voice chats are all limited, and OpenAI's pricing page says your chats can be used to train its models unless you opt out.
  • Claude: best for long writing and documents. Anthropic's app is built around drafting and editing, reading PDFs, screenshots and photos, voice conversations, and connections to Google Drive, Gmail and Calendar. The limit is usage: every plan, free included, has caps that reset on a rolling five-hour window, and your phone draws from the same pool as Claude on the web and desktop.
  • Gemini: best if you live in Google. It connects to Gmail, Calendar, Google Photos and YouTube, and Gemini Live lets you share your camera or screen and talk through what you see. Photo generation and editing with Nano Banana 2 is in the app, but Google's listing reserves video generation for its paid AI plans and Workspace. It needs iOS 18.2 or later.
  • Copilot: best for Microsoft 365 work. Microsoft calls it "the AI built for work." It connects to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams, and drafts documents and decks from your phone. Two limits: it requires iOS 26 or later, and the deepest organizational features need a company Microsoft 365 Copilot subscription.
  • Grok: best for what's happening right now. It answers with direct access to X and the web, and adds image and video generation through Grok Imagine plus a voice mode. The weakness is the flip side of the strength: a stream of fresh posts isn't a vetted source, so check anything important somewhere else.

Pick one as your default and give it a month before adding a second. If you're stuck between the big three, our ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini comparison goes task by task.

Best iPhone apps for research, voice, writing and images

  • Research: Perplexity. Every answer comes with cited sources, and the app adds Pro Search and Deep Research for bigger questions, plus voice input. It's the one to open when you need to show where a fact came from. The limit: a citation proves a page exists, not that the answer read it correctly. Our guide to using Perplexity covers the checking habit.
  • Voice: Wispr Flow. A voice keyboard that works inside other apps (Messages, email, Slack, even ChatGPT) and turns rambling speech into clean, formatted text. The free tier has a weekly word limit that Pro removes. If you want AI voices for narration rather than dictation, ElevenLabs has its own iPhone app for that.
  • Writing: Grammarly. The Grammarly keyboard checks spelling, grammar and tone in whatever app you're typing in, and can rewrite a whole message in a couple of taps, in 20-plus languages. Vocabulary and clarity suggestions and tone rewrites are Premium only. For long drafts, Claude is the better tool; Grammarly polishes what you type everywhere else.
  • Images and video: Adobe Firefly. Firefly's iPhone app puts Adobe's own models next to partner models from Google, OpenAI, Luma and Runway, for images, short videos and edits like background removal. Adobe says its own models are trained on licensed content. Generation runs on credits, though signed-in users without a subscription get some free generations each day. What's built into the iPhone: Siri, ChatGPT and the Action button

Before installing anything, check what iOS already does. On iOS 27, Apple Intelligence runs on the iPhone 15 Pro and Pro Max and on every iPhone 16 model or later.

  • ChatGPT inside Siri. Open Settings, tap Siri, then ChatGPT, and follow the setup (with the Siri AI beta it sits under Extensions). You don't need a ChatGPT account; sign in only if you want requests saved to your ChatGPT history. Apple says that without an account, OpenAI must not store your requests or use them to train its models. One of Apple's own examples is asking Siri to have ChatGPT summarize a PDF you have open in Files.
  • Siri AI. Apple's new conversational Siri, with its own Siri app, onscreen awareness and your personal context, is in beta. It starts in English, isn't initially available in the EU, and Apple notes that usage limits may apply.
  • Write with Siri. Siri AI can draft from scratch, summarize, or give feedback on what you've written, which covers a lot of what people install a writing app for.
  • The Action button. On models that have one, open Settings, tap Action Button, swipe to Shortcut, then tap the option below it and choose an app to open. Pick your main assistant, and a press and hold launches it. Apple's guide warns that the button does nothing until you pick that specific option. Visual Intelligence is on the same screen if you'd rather point the camera and ask.

The built-in tools are fine for quick requests. For long chats, files and ongoing projects, you'll still want a dedicated app.

FAQ

What is the best AI app for iPhone?

For most people it's ChatGPT: it handles voice, photos, images and everyday questions, and Apple connects it to Siri. Choose Claude for long writing, Gemini for Google apps, Copilot for Microsoft 365 and Perplexity for research with sources. To get better at using whichever you pick, Iro AI teaches the skill in five-minute lessons.

Is ChatGPT built into the iPhone?

Partly. On iOS 27, Siri can pass requests to ChatGPT once you turn on the ChatGPT extension in Settings, under Siri. It works on the iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max and iPhone 16 models or later. You don't need a ChatGPT account, but signing in saves your requests to your ChatGPT history.

Which iPhones support Apple Intelligence?

On iOS 27, Apple lists the iPhone 15 Pro, the iPhone 15 Pro Max and any iPhone 16 model or later. Siri AI, the new conversational Siri, is in beta, starts in English and isn't initially available in the EU on iPhone.

How do I put ChatGPT on the iPhone Action button?

Open Settings, tap Action Button, swipe to Shortcut, then tap the option below it and choose to open an app. Pick ChatGPT or whichever assistant you use most. After that, pressing and holding the Action button opens it. Apple notes the button does nothing until you choose that specific option.

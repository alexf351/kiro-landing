Cosmo alternatives: 7 apps to try instead of CodeSignal's Cosmo

What are the best Cosmo alternatives?

It depends on why you are leaving. Cosmo is a broad, free-to-learn app from CodeSignal that covers 200+ career skills, so the right alternative is usually a more focused one:

  • To get good at using AI tools: Iro AI (ours), built only for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and prompting.
  • For coding or data: DataCamp, Sololearn or Mimo.
  • For math, logic and how AI works: Brilliant.
  • For a recognised certificate: Coursera, including DeepLearning.AI's courses.
  • For free foundations: Khan Academy.

Why do people look for a Cosmo alternative?

Cosmo has real strengths. It comes from CodeSignal, Inc., an established San Francisco company; its App Store listing says every course is free; and purchases go through Apple or Google, so cancelling is simple. Our full Cosmo review covers who makes it, how it teaches and what its terms say.

People look elsewhere for four reasons:

  • Breadth over depth. Cosmo's listing claims 200+ skills across GenAI, business, coding and data. VentureBeat's launch coverage said nearly a third of the content was about generative AI, which leaves most of the catalogue on other subjects.
  • Price. The free tier runs on energy that practice uses up. Cosmo+ removes the limit, and the US App Store lists four Unlimited Access purchases: $24.99, $12.99, $149.99 and $78.00, without saying which period each covers. Launch coverage priced it at $24.99 a month or $149.99 a year.
  • Platform. The App Store listing is for iPhone, and there is an Android app on Google Play. Neither CodeSignal's launch release nor its listing mentions a web version.
  • A specific goal. If you know you want coding, data science, AI tools or a certificate, a specialist app goes further than a generalist one.

2 to 7: the other Cosmo alternatives

2. DataCamp: for data science and coding

DataCamp says it has 800+ courses and more than 20 million learners, built around hands-on exercises in the browser, with an iPhone and Android app for short daily practice. It goes far deeper than Cosmo on Python, SQL, R and machine learning, and it has beginner AI courses that need no code. The first chapter of every course is free. Pick it if the coding and data parts of Cosmo were what you liked. More in Iro AI vs DataCamp.

3. Sololearn: bite-size coding, with AI on top

Short coding lessons with a large community; its Learn AI content sits in the top tier. PRO is $12.99 a month or $69.99 a year. Pick it if you want to write code on your phone.

4. Mimo: a gentle start in coding

Interactive coding lessons in Python and web development, with a free basic plan and paid upgrades. Pick it if you are completely new to code.

5. Brilliant: math, logic and how AI works

Brilliant's interactive lessons on math, logic and computer science are some of the best built in the category. It costs $24.99 a month or $119.99 a year in the App Store. Pick it if you want to understand the ideas behind AI rather than use the tools.

6. Coursera and DeepLearning.AI: courses with certificates

University-style video courses, including Andrew Ng's, with certificates employers recognise. Coursera Plus is $59 a month or $399 a year. Pick it if a credential matters and you can commit hours a week. Our guide to whether AI certifications are worth it helps you decide.

7. Khan Academy: free foundations

The nonprofit covers math, science and computing for free, with its Khanmigo tutor for AI help. Pick it if price is the main reason you are leaving Cosmo.

How do you choose a Cosmo alternative?

  • You want to use ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini better at work: Iro AI.
  • You want to code or work with data: DataCamp, Sololearn or Mimo.
  • You want to understand how AI works: Brilliant or Khan Academy.
  • You need a certificate: Coursera.
  • You liked Cosmo's spread of business and soft skills: stay on Cosmo's free courses. No app here matches that breadth.

FAQ

What is the best alternative to Cosmo?

For learning to use AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, Iro AI is the focused alternative. For coding and data, DataCamp or Sololearn. For math and how AI works, Brilliant. For free foundations, Khan Academy.

Is there a free alternative to Cosmo?

Khan Academy is entirely free. Iro AI is free to start, with every path open and a battery that gives you a full lesson a day. DataCamp's free tier includes the first chapter of every course. Cosmo's own courses are free too, limited by energy.

Is Cosmo cheaper than Iro AI?

Cosmo's courses are free, but its paid tier costs more. Launch coverage priced Cosmo+ at $24.99 a month or $149.99 a year. Iro AI Pro is $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial on the annual plan.

Is Cosmo good for learning AI?

Cosmo covers GenAI, including prompting and using ChatGPT at work, but at launch that was roughly a third of a catalogue that also spans business, coding and data. If AI skills are your main goal, a focused app goes deeper.

What app is like Cosmo but for coding?

DataCamp, Sololearn and Mimo all teach coding in short sessions. DataCamp goes deepest on data science and Python; Mimo is the gentlest start for complete beginners.

Google AI Essentials alternatives: practice-first options in 2026

Google AI Essentials is a solid overview. It is also paid, and only one of its five courses is about prompting. Here are the alternatives, grouped by what you actually want out of it.

Key takeaways

  • Coursera's own page says Google AI Essentials cannot be taken for free: in the US and Canada it is $49 a month after a 7-day trial.
  • If you want a free certificate-style course, OpenAI Academy and Anthropic's AI Fluency course are both free and short.
  • If you want foundations rather than tools, Elements of AI is free and needs no programming for Part 1.
  • If the gap is practice, pick something you do every day rather than another course you watch: that is what Iro AI is for.

The short answer

If you want a free course with a certificate, start with OpenAI Academy or Anthropic's AI Fluency course. If you want foundations, take Elements of AI. If what you are missing is practice, a course is the wrong shape, and a daily practice app like Iro AI fits better.

Google AI Essentials is a reasonable overview from a name employers recognise. The two reasons people look elsewhere are cost and depth: Coursera's own page says it is not free, and prompting gets one course out of five.

What Google AI Essentials covers

On Coursera it is a series of five short courses:

  1. Introduction to AI
  2. Maximize Productivity With AI Tools
  3. Discover the Art of Prompting
  4. Use AI Responsibly
  5. Stay Ahead of the AI Curve

Coursera says each course includes hands-on activities and graded assessments, and you finish with a certificate from Google. The stated length varies by page: Google's own page says under five hours, Coursera's description says under ten, and the five course lengths listed on Coursera add up to about eight.

Cost. Coursera's FAQ answers "Can I take the course for free?" with "No, you cannot take this course for free." In the US and Canada it is $49 a month after a 7-day free trial, and Coursera offers financial aid. The page still shows an "Enroll for free" button, which is the trial.

AI For Everyone: what it is, and what it is not

AI For Everyone, from DeepLearning.AI and taught by Andrew Ng, is the other course people usually compare. Coursera lists it at about seven hours over four weeks: what AI is, building AI projects, building AI in your company, and AI and society. Each week ends in a quiz.

It is a course about understanding AI as a manager or decision-maker. Its syllabus lists no hands-on prompting, so if you finish it and still don't feel comfortable using ChatGPT or Claude for real work, that is by design rather than a gap in your effort. You can enroll free; the certificate and graded work need the paid option.

How to choose

  • You need a certificate for a résumé or an employer: Google AI Essentials is the most recognisable name. If cost is the blocker, OpenAI Academy and Anthropic's AI Fluency both give a certificate of completion for free. OpenAI notes its certificates are not a formal credential.
  • You want to understand AI before using it: Elements of AI, Part 1. It is free and needs no programming.
  • You want to get better at prompting specifically: the Vanderbilt course goes deepest of the courses here.
  • You have taken a course and still don't use AI much: the missing piece is repetition, not information. A few minutes of real practice every day builds the habit a course can't. That is the job Iro AI does, and you can start free on iPhone or at app.tryiro.com.

What to take after AI For Everyone

AI For Everyone explains what AI can do for an organisation. The natural next step is learning to use the tools yourself. Take OpenAI Academy's AI Foundations if you use ChatGPT, or Anthropic's AI Fluency course if you use Claude, then practise daily so it sticks. FAQ

Is Google AI Essentials free?

No. Coursera's own page says you cannot take it for free. In the US and Canada it costs $49 a month after a 7-day free trial, and Coursera offers financial aid.

What is the best free alternative to Google AI Essentials?

OpenAI Academy and Anthropic's AI Fluency course are both free and give a certificate of completion. Elements of AI is free if you want foundations rather than tool skills.

Does AI For Everyone teach prompting?

Not as hands-on practice. Its syllabus covers what AI is, AI projects, AI in companies and AI and society, with a quiz each week, and lists no prompting exercises.

What should I take after AI For Everyone?

A short course on the tool you actually use, such as OpenAI Academy for ChatGPT or Anthropic's AI Fluency for Claude, followed by daily practice so the skill sticks.

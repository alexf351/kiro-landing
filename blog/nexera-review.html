Is Nexera legit, or a scam?

Nexera is a legitimate company selling a real AI course app, and its refund terms are better than most in this category. The lessons cover tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney and Zapier in short, bite-sized sessions, and you get a certificate at the end. Website purchases come with a 28-day money-back guarantee. The thing to watch is the purchase itself: Nexera sells through its website as well as the app stores, with trial offers that turn into a full-price plan unless you cancel in time.

If you have not paid yet, decide how you are buying before you enter a card, for the reasons below. If you already have and want out, go straight to how to cancel Nexera.

Who makes Nexera?

Nexera's Terms of Use name its operator as EMBERTECH LIMITED, a company registered in Cyprus under number HE 458701, with an office in Nicosia. The App Store lists the seller as EmberTech Ltd, and the Google Play listing gives the same Nicosia address. Support runs through support@nexera-app.com.

The terms name a few more companies you may see on a receipt or statement:

  • Authorised resellers: Syncrotech Solutions, Inc. (Wyoming, USA) and AdvancePro Solutions FZ-LLC (Dubai, UAE).
  • Merchants of record: DMORE Merchant of Record Ltd (Ireland) and Paddle. The terms say their role is limited to invoicing, collecting payments and taxes, and that EmberTech runs the service itself.

One more name: the web app at app.nexera.cc carries a copyright line for Fortunai Limited, and its privacy policy names Fortunai as the data controller. Nexera does not explain the link between the two companies on the pages we read. If an unfamiliar name appears on your card, check your Nexera confirmation email before assuming it is something else.

The app itself is young. The iPhone version, listed as Nexera: AI Tools Learning App, first went live on the App Store in April 2026, so there is only a few months of track record to judge it by. And it is unrelated to other businesses called Nexera, which is worth knowing if you search the name.

What is Nexera, and what do you get?

Nexera is an AI-skills app from Cyprus-based EmberTech Ltd that teaches popular AI tools in short lessons.

According to its App Store listing, the app is built around a 28-Day Structured AI Challenge and a set of courses, including AI literacy, AI fundamentals, a Claude course, working faster with ChatGPT, prompt engineering (ChatGPT, Midjourney, Perplexity, NotebookLM), workflow automation (Zapier, n8n, Microsoft 365 Copilot, GitHub Copilot) and creative tools (Canva, Sora and others). It adds hands-on exercises, a prompt library and a certificate when you finish. Nexera's FAQ says the certificate confirms completion and is not an accredited qualification or a guarantee of employment.

The listing also includes a course called AI-Driven Income Growth. Nexera's own terms say it does not guarantee any income increase or career advancement, and its website says outcomes vary by learner. Read that course as a skills course, not a promise.

We have not paid for Nexera, so we are not rating lesson quality here. Reviewers on Trustpilot split on it: some praise clear, easy lessons, while a few describe the courses as mostly slides and more theory than practice. The honest test takes one session: are you writing prompts and getting feedback, or reading and copying? Only one of those builds the skill.

How much does Nexera cost?

Nexera is free to download. On the US App Store it lists these in-app purchases:

  • Monthly subscription: $9.99
  • Yearly subscription: $69.99 or $59.99
  • Annual promo subscription: $59.99 or $39.99

The listing does not say which offer you will see. Those are billed by Apple and behave like any other App Store subscription.

The website works differently. Nexera's FAQ says the cost "depends on the offer and subscription terms displayed during the sign-up process", and its terms list possible plan lengths from one week to a year. We did not go through checkout, so we cannot quote web prices; several Trustpilot reviewers mention paying around 30 euros or pounds a month. What the terms do say about web plans:

  • Trials turn into the plan you picked. When a trial ends, you are charged for the subscription you chose at sign-up unless you cancel first.
  • Plans renew automatically until you cancel, and you have to cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the trial or period to avoid the next charge.
  • Prices can change at any time, effective from your next renewal.

This is the same web-funnel model used by Coursiv and similar AI course apps. None of it is hidden; it is all in the terms. But the number on your first receipt may not be the number on your second, so find the renewal price on the checkout page before you pay.

Can you get a refund from Nexera?

This is where Nexera is better than many apps like it. For purchases on its website, Nexera's terms offer a 28-day money-back guarantee: you can ask for a full refund of payments made within 28 calendar days of your purchase time. The clock starts when your trial payment (or, with no trial, your first subscription payment) is charged, not when you sign up or first open the app. You ask by emailing support@nexera-app.com from the address on your account.

The terms set no usage condition on that guarantee, unlike Coursiv's, which pays out only if you used the app enough (see how to cancel Coursiv). Nexera says refunds are issued within two business days and can take up to ten to reach your card. After 28 days, the terms say web purchases are final except where local law gives you more.

App Store and Google Play purchases are different. Nexera's terms say it cannot refund in-app purchases at all, because Apple and Google process them. You ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com or Google through your Play order history, and they decide under their own rules.

What do Nexera reviews and complaints say?

Nexera rates well on every platform we checked: 4.4 stars from more than 800 ratings on the US App Store, 4.4 on Google Play with about 1,000 reviews and 50K+ downloads, and a TrustScore of 4.4 from about 210 reviews on Trustpilot, where Nexera claimed its profile in February 2026 and replies to negative reviews.

The pattern in the reviews:

  • The praise is mostly about customer support. Many five-star Trustpilot reviews say a refund or a cancellation was handled quickly, sometimes the same day.
  • The complaints are mostly about billing. About 9% of Trustpilot reviews are one star, and the ones we read describe a renewal after a trial that the customer had not expected, or charges they say continued after cancelling. One five-star review describes a trial renewal at about five times the first charge, which support then reversed. On the App Store, one reviewer reports being charged twice.

Read that together and the picture is consistent: the trial-to-full-price renewal catches people out, and support usually fixes it when asked inside the refund window. Many reviews also describe refund requests, so expect that to colour the overall score. Sort by newest and read the low ratings as well as the high ones.

If you still want to try Nexera

  1. Decide where you are buying. Through the App Store, Apple bills you and you cancel in Settings. On the website, you get Nexera's 28-day refund but have to cancel in your Nexera account.
  2. On the website, screenshot the checkout page showing the trial length, the plan you are moving to and its price.
  3. Set a reminder three days before the trial ends, and another before day 28 if you are unsure. The 24-hour cutoff is where most surprise renewals happen.
  4. Remember that deleting the app does not cancel. Nexera's terms and FAQ both say so, and neither does deleting your account.

What Iro is not

Worth stating first, because it saves you time if we are the wrong fit.

  • Not a classroom platform. There is no teacher dashboard, no rostering, no assignment flow and no LMS integration. If you need to assign, monitor and grade a class, a purpose-built school product will serve you better.
  • Not a district purchase. Iro is sold as an individual subscription through the App Store. There is no site or volume licence at this time.
  • Not for elementary. Iro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. High school and up is the honest range.
  • Not a computer science course. Iro teaches people to use AI well, not to build models. There is no coding requirement and no maths prerequisite.

Where it does fit

Two places, and they are the two we hear about most.

Your own fluency

The most common educator use. Five minutes a day on prompting, verification and tool choice, so you are ahead of the questions your students are already asking. Every path is open free.

Older students, going further

A high school or university student who has finished your unit and wants to keep going has somewhere to go that is structured rather than a YouTube rabbit hole.

A starting-point measure

The free AI IQ test needs no account, so it works as an anonymous pre- and post-unit check that scores judgment rather than definitions.

How the free tier actually works

Not a limited trial. Every one of the 29 learning paths and 477 lessons is open on the free tier, with no credit card to start.

A daily Battery paces you to one new lesson a day and recharges overnight. Reviews, replays, the daily challenge and the weekly AI news quiz never consume a charge, and wrong answers cost nothing — you always finish a lesson you have started. Streaks, XP, the 16 ranks and earning certificates are all free.

Iro Pro removes the daily pacing and adds unlimited AI coaching, a prompt lab, an image lab, timed duels, custom learning paths on any topic, and claiming a certificate with your name on it. It is $49.99 a year — about $4.17 a month, with a 7-day free trial — or $9.99 a month. Subscriptions are managed through Apple.

Questions educators ask

Is Iro AI suitable for classroom use?

Iro is built for individual learners rather than as a classroom management system. There is no teacher dashboard, no student rostering and no LMS integration, so it does not slot into a class the way a purpose-built school platform does. Where it works well is a teacher building their own AI fluency, and older students who want to go further than a unit allows.

What age is Iro AI appropriate for?

Iro is not directed at children under 13, and our privacy policy states that we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. In practice that makes it suitable for high school students, university students and educators rather than elementary classrooms.

Is there a free version teachers can try?

Yes, and it is not a limited trial. Every one of the 29 learning paths and 477 lessons is open on the free tier, with no credit card required. A daily Battery paces you to one new lesson a day and recharges overnight, and reviews, replays and daily challenges never consume it.

Can I use the AI IQ test with students?

Yes. It is 10 questions, takes about two minutes, and requires no account or signup, which makes it usable as an anonymous pre- and post-unit check. It tests practical judgment rather than definitions, so it maps to what an AI literacy unit actually teaches.

Do you offer school or district licensing?

Not currently. Iro is sold as an individual subscription through the App Store: $49.99 a year, which includes a 7-day free trial, or $9.99 a month. There is no volume or site licence at this time.

What does Iro actually teach?

Practical AI use rather than AI theory: prompting, judging whether an answer is trustworthy, spotting hallucinations, comparing model outputs, and choosing the right tool for a task. It covers ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, prompt engineering, AI agents, automation and creative AI across 29 paths, 477 lessons and 3,000+ exercises.

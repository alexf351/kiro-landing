## Why marketers need AI fluency

AI can accelerate marketing work, but vague prompts often create generic copy. The marketing skill is knowing what context matters: audience, offer, differentiation, channel, tone, and success criteria.
## Marketing skills Iro reinforces

- Prompting for audience-specific content.

- Turning research into messaging and briefs.
- Using AI as a collaborator while keeping human judgment.

## What's the best way for a marketer to learn AI?

**Practice on your own marketing tasks a few minutes a day, instead of collecting prompt packs.** What separates useful AI output from generic copy is the context a good brief would have: who the audience is, what the offer is, what makes you different, the channel, the tone and what success looks like. Then you critique the draft and ask for a sharper version.

1. **Context first.** Prompts that include audience, offer, channel and constraints.
2. **Research and positioning.** Summarize reviews and competitor claims, then check every fact against the source.
3. **Briefs and angles.** Turn messy notes into a structured brief and several distinct angles.
4. **Quality control.** Spot generic phrasing and invented claims, and ask for specific revisions.
5. **One reusable workflow.** Save the prompt sequence you use every week.

Iro turns these into five-minute exercises where you write or fix the prompt and get feedback. It doesn't teach marketing strategy, run your ad platforms or publish content for you.

## How can college students learn to use AI responsibly? Is there an app?

**Yes. Iro's AI for School path teaches responsible AI use in 15 lessons of about five minutes each**, on iPhone and in any browser. It treats AI as a tutor and study partner, not an essay machine, and every lesson ends in a hands-on exercise with instant feedback.

- **Learn, don't outsource:** have AI explain and quiz you instead of doing the work (Use AI Like a Personal Tutor, Get Unstuck on Homework).
- **Check what it says:** test its claims against real sources (Research Without Getting Fooled, Find Better Sources).
- **Keep your own thinking:** brainstorm and get feedback while the ideas and words stay yours (Brainstorm Essays Ethically, Improve Your Writing).
- **Study actively:** study guides, flashcards and recall practice (Turn Class Notes Into Study Guides, Make Flashcards and Practice Quizzes, Study With Active Recall).

Your school's and instructor's AI policy always comes first. Free to start.

AI tools are becoming part of school, internships, and early-career work. Students need more than shortcuts. They need to know how to ask, verify, revise, and use AI ethically and effectively.

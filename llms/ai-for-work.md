# Learn AI for work in 5-minute sessions.

## Who Iro is for

- **For:** professionals in any role who want to use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity for writing, research, analysis and planning, in five-minute sessions.
- **What it fixes:** treating AI like a magic search box. You practice giving clear goals, context and constraints, then reviewing what comes back for errors.
- **Not a fit if:** you need a company-wide training rollout with an admin dashboard, an accredited certificate, or a course on building AI systems.

## Why AI at work needs training

AI adoption at work fails when teams treat AI like a magic search box. Useful results require clear goals, context, constraints, review, and an understanding of privacy and verification.
## Work skills Iro covers

- Prompting for professional outputs and structured drafts.
- Reviewing AI-generated analysis and summaries.
- Understanding agents, automation, and workflow design.
- Building daily practice instead of one-off experimentation.

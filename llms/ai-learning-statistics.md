# AI learning statistics 2026: how beginners actually learn AI

> Original data from 339 learners who finished at least one lesson in Iro, an app that teaches AI skills in short daily lessons on iPhone and the web, between April 30 and November 17, 2026.

**Canonical page:** https://tryiro.com/ai-learning-statistics
**Data (CSV, CC BY 4.0):** https://tryiro.com/data/ai-learning-statistics-2026.csv
**Last updated:** 2026-11-17

## Key findings

- Beginners who asked an AI tutor a question on their first day were 1.7x as likely to come back (62% vs 36%).
- Learners who finished two lessons on day one came back 58% of the time, against 42% after one lesson.
- 37% of people who finished a first AI lesson never finished a second.
- In timed quizzes, answers given in under 8 seconds were right 71% of the time, against 59% for slower answers.
- When people could build a lesson on anything, 57% chose a topic other than AI.

## 1. Asking the AI tutor on day one: 62% came back, vs 36%

Beginners who asked Iro's AI tutor a question on their first day came back to learn on a later day 62% of the time. Learners who never asked the tutor anything came back 36% of the time, so first-day askers were 1.7x as likely to return. The question the most learners asked was "Why was mine wrong?", ahead of "Give an example" and "Quiz me".

Sample: 50 learners who asked on day one and 234 who never asked, all with a first lesson before November 10, 2026. This is a correlation.

## 2. Two lessons on day one: 58% came back, vs 42% after one

Sample: 120 and 204 learners, first lesson before November 10, 2026.

## 3. 37% never finish a second AI lesson

37% of people who finished their first AI lesson never finished a second one (126 of 339). 34% reached five lessons, 18% reached ten and 6% reached 25. Learners who did return usually came back the very next day (median gap: one day). Each lesson is counted once per learner.

## 4. Quick answers were right 71% of the time, vs 59% for slower ones

In 15-second head-to-head quiz duels, answers given in under 8 seconds were right 71% of the time; answers that took 8 seconds or more were right 59% of the time. Sample: 544 answers from 25 players, timeouts excluded. Small sample: an early pattern.

## 5. Given free rein, 57% chose a topic other than AI

When learners could build a custom lesson on any topic, 57% chose something other than AI, such as cooking, personal finance, fitness or job interviews. 73% of those lessons were set to beginner level. Sample: 67 custom lessons from 23 learners, tagged by keyword. Small sample: an early pattern.

## Also in the data

Weekend days averaged about 19% fewer active learners than weekdays.

## Method

- Source: Iro's own lesson, tutor, duel and custom-lesson records, aggregated. No personal data.
- Learners: accounts that finished at least one lesson between April 30 and November 17, 2026 (339). Replays excluded.
- Came back: finished a lesson on a later calendar day than the first one. Return comparisons only include learners whose first lesson was at least a week before the data was pulled.
- Asked on day one: asked the AI tutor a question on or before the day of the first lesson.
- Excluded: the founder's own and test accounts, and the app store review account.

## Limitations

Self-selected learners of one app on iPhone and the web, not a random sample. No demographics. Return rates are correlations, not proof of cause. Findings 4 and 5 rest on fewer than 30 people each. Refreshed quarterly.

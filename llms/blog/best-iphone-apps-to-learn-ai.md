# The best iPhone apps to learn AI in 2026

> If you want to learn AI on an iPhone, the best picks are Iro AI for daily hands-on prompting practice, Learn AI by Sololearn for structured courses with a prompt playground, Synapse for short lessons with labs inside AI tools, and Brilliant if you want the math under the hood. Newer apps like Nexera, EWA: Learn AI Skills and Learn AI: AI Tools Mastery lean on guided courses and projects. Some apps that show up in "best of" lists aren't on the App Store yet, so check before you pick.

## Key takeaways

- Iro AI is built for daily prompting practice: five-minute lessons where you write and fix real prompts and get feedback.
- Learn AI by Sololearn and Synapse also have you practice prompts on iPhone; Brilliant teaches how models work rather than how to use them.
- Nexera, EWA: Learn AI Skills and Learn AI: AI Tools Mastery are newer course-style apps; their monthly prices on the US App Store run from $9.99 to $29.99.
- Duolingo doesn't teach AI. It uses AI inside its language and math courses.
- Unrot, a free AI learning app that appears in several "best" lists, is on Android and the web only so far.

## Why pick Iro AI for daily practice?

**Iro AI is a five-minute AI learning game.** Each lesson has you write and fix real prompts and spot hallucinations, with streaks, XP, ranks, a Daily Challenge and timed duels to keep the habit going. It has 34 learning paths and 522 lessons, including paths for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Cursor and for using AI at work. The free tier opens every lesson and paces you to one new lesson a day. Pro is $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year, and you can cancel anytime in your Apple settings.

**Pick something else if** you want an accredited certificate, a long video course, or the math behind models. Iro is on iPhone and in any browser at app.tryiro.com, with no Android app yet.

## When should you pick something else?

- **Learn AI by Sololearn, for structured courses:** Sololearn's separate Learn AI app turns AI tools and concepts into short course modules, with a playground for generative AI tools and feedback on your prompts. It suits people who like a syllabus and a clear finish line. It is free to download and rated 4.4 from 49 ratings; the MAX plan is $11.99 a week or $34.99 a month on the App Store.
- **Synapse, for lessons with labs inside AI tools:** Synapse pairs five-minute lessons and quizzes with hands-on labs in tools like Claude and ChatGPT and a Prompt Lab that scores your prompts. It runs on iPhone and iPad, with a 14-day trial before a weekly subscription.
- **Nexera, for a 28-day AI challenge:** Nexera (listed on the App Store as Nexera: Learn AI Tools, from EmberTech) is built around a 28-day step-by-step challenge, with extra courses on ChatGPT, Claude, prompt engineering, automation and earning with AI, plus hands-on exercises and a prompt library. It is rated 4.4 from about 1,200 ratings. Subscriptions are $9.99 a month or $59.99 to $69.99 a year, with promo annual prices listed from $39.99. It runs on iPhone and iPad. Our [Nexera review](/blog/nexera-review) covers it in full.
- **EWA: Learn AI Skills, for project-based courses:** EWA's AI app lists more than 100 courses and 1,000+ lessons of 5 to 10 minutes, including courses where you finish a real project such as a landing page or a business plan. It is new, rated 4.3 from 11 ratings. Premium is $19.99 a month or $99.99 a year and Max is $29.99 a month or $139.99 a year, with sale prices listed lower. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Apple silicon Macs and Vision Pro.
- **Learn AI: AI Tools Mastery, for guided tool lessons:** This app builds a custom learning path of guided lessons on tools like Claude, Gemini, Sora and Cursor, and adds AI image generation and a chat assistant on its paid tier. It is rated 4.4 from 148 ratings. Its listing shows Unlimited Access purchases from $14.99 to $199.99 without naming the plan lengths, so check the term at checkout. It is built for iPhone.
- **Coursiv, for guided AI tool challenges:** Coursiv runs challenge-style courses on popular AI tools on auto-renewing 1-week, 4-week and 12-week plans. Read the subscription terms before you start; our [Coursiv reviews and pricing](/blog/iro-ai-vs-coursiv#reviews-pricing) summary and [Coursiv cancellation guide](/blog/cancel-coursiv) cover them.
- **Brilliant, for how AI works:** Brilliant teaches the math and logic behind neural networks through interactive problems. It's the deepest pick here for how models work, and the least about using AI tools day to day.

## What do iPhone AI learning apps cost?

Most AI learning apps are free to download and charge for a subscription inside the app. These are the monthly prices the US App Store showed on November 21, 2026:

- **Iro AI:** $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a free tier that opens every lesson.
- **Nexera:** $9.99 a month, or $59.99 to $69.99 a year.
- **EWA: Learn AI Skills:** $19.99 a month for Premium, $29.99 for Max.
- **Learn AI by Sololearn:** $34.99 a month or $11.99 a week for MAX.
- **Learn AI: AI Tools Mastery:** $14.99 to $199.99 for Unlimited Access, with plan lengths not stated.

Weekly and four-week plans add up faster than they look: four weeks is 13 billing periods a year, not 12. Before you pay, check the renewal price and term on the checkout screen, and set a reminder before any trial ends.

No. Duolingo uses AI inside its language and math courses, but it has no course that teaches you to use AI. If you want the Duolingo format for AI skills, see [apps like Duolingo for AI](/blog/apps-like-duolingo-for-ai) and [does Duolingo teach AI?](/blog/does-duolingo-teach-ai)

## FAQ

**What is the best free iPhone app to learn AI?**

Iro AI's free tier opens every lesson and paces you to one new lesson a day, which is enough to build a daily habit. Brilliant is also free with daily limits, but it teaches how AI works rather than how to use it.

**How much do iPhone apps to learn AI cost?**

Most are free to download with a paid subscription. On the US App Store in November 2026, Iro AI and Nexera start at $9.99 a month, EWA: Learn AI Skills at $19.99 a month, and Sololearn's Learn AI MAX at $34.99 a month or $11.99 a week. Iro AI also has a free tier that opens every lesson.

**Can you learn AI in 5 minutes a day?**

You can build practical AI skills, like writing clear prompts and checking AI answers, in five minutes a day if you practice rather than just read. You won't learn to build models that way.

**Is there a Duolingo for AI on iPhone?**

Yes. Iro AI uses the same streak, XP and rank format as Duolingo, but for AI skills like prompting and spotting hallucinations.

**Do I need to code to learn AI on my phone?**

No. Iro AI, Synapse, Nexera, EWA: Learn AI Skills and Coursiv are built for people who don't code, and Learn AI by Sololearn is separate from Sololearn's coding courses. You only need code if you want to build AI models or apps.

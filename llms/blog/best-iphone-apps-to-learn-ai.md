# The best iPhone apps to learn AI in 2026

> If you want to learn AI on an iPhone, the best picks are Iro AI for daily hands-on prompting practice, Learn AI by Sololearn for structured courses with a prompt playground, Synapse for short lessons with labs inside AI tools, and Brilliant if you want the math under the hood. Some apps that show up in "best of" lists aren't on the App Store yet, so check before you pick.

## Key takeaways

- Iro AI is built for daily prompting practice: five-minute lessons where you write and fix real prompts and get feedback.
- Learn AI by Sololearn and Synapse also have you practice prompts on iPhone; Brilliant teaches how models work rather than how to use them.
- Duolingo doesn't teach AI. It uses AI inside its language and math courses.
- Unrot, a free AI learning app that appears in several "best" lists, is on Android and the web only so far.

## Why pick Iro AI for daily practice?

**Iro AI is a five-minute AI learning game.** Each lesson has you write and fix real prompts and spot hallucinations, with streaks, XP, ranks, a Daily Challenge and timed duels to keep the habit going. It has 34 learning paths and 522 lessons, including paths for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Cursor and for using AI at work. The free tier opens every lesson and paces you to one new lesson a day.

**Pick something else if** you want an accredited certificate, a long video course, or the math behind models. Iro is on iPhone and in any browser at app.tryiro.com, with no Android app yet.

## When should you pick something else?

- **Learn AI by Sololearn, for structured courses:** Sololearn's separate Learn AI app turns AI tools and concepts into short course modules, with a playground for generative AI tools and feedback on your prompts. It suits people who like a syllabus and a clear finish line. It is free to download, with a paid MAX plan.
- **Synapse, for lessons with labs inside AI tools:** Synapse pairs five-minute lessons and quizzes with hands-on labs in tools like Claude and ChatGPT and a Prompt Lab that scores your prompts. It runs on iPhone and iPad, with a 14-day trial before a weekly subscription.
- **Coursiv, for guided AI tool challenges:** Coursiv runs challenge-style courses on popular AI tools. Read the subscription terms before you start; our [Coursiv cancellation guide](/blog/cancel-coursiv) covers them.
- **Brilliant, for how AI works:** Brilliant teaches the math and logic behind neural networks through interactive problems. It's the deepest pick here for how models work, and the least about using AI tools day to day.

## Does Duolingo teach AI?

No. Duolingo uses AI inside its language and math courses, but it has no course that teaches you to use AI. If you want the Duolingo format for AI skills, see [apps like Duolingo for AI](/blog/apps-like-duolingo-for-ai) and [does Duolingo teach AI?](/blog/does-duolingo-teach-ai)

## FAQ

**What is the best free iPhone app to learn AI?**

Iro AI's free tier opens every lesson and paces you to one new lesson a day, which is enough to build a daily habit. Brilliant is also free with daily limits, but it teaches how AI works rather than how to use it.

**Can you learn AI in 5 minutes a day?**

You can build practical AI skills, like writing clear prompts and checking AI answers, in five minutes a day if you practice rather than just read. You won't learn to build models that way.

**Is there a Duolingo for AI on iPhone?**

Yes. Iro AI uses the same streak, XP and rank format as Duolingo, but for AI skills like prompting and spotting hallucinations.

**Do I need to code to learn AI on my phone?**

No. Iro AI, Synapse and Coursiv are built for people who don't code, and Learn AI by Sololearn is separate from Sololearn's coding courses. You only need code if you want to build AI models or apps.

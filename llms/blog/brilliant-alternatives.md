## Why do people look for Brilliant alternatives?

Brilliant is excellent at interactive math, logic and computer-science foundations. People usually look elsewhere for one of three reasons: the price ($24.99 a month or $119.99 a year in the App Store), wanting to learn how to _use_ AI tools rather than the math behind them, or wanting something for a child rather than an adult.

## FAQ

**What is the best alternative to Brilliant?**

It depends on what you want from Brilliant. For learning to use AI tools, Iro AI. For free math and science, Khan Academy. For coding, Sololearn or Mimo. For data science, DataCamp.

**Is there a free Brilliant alternative?**

Yes. Khan Academy and Elements of AI are free. Iro AI is free to start, with every lesson open and one new lesson a day, and Pro has a 7-day free trial.

**What is the best gamified app to learn AI?**

Iro AI is built like Duolingo for AI: short daily lessons, XP, streaks, ranks and duels, and practice on real prompts with feedback. It runs on iPhone and the web. Synapse and Sololearn also teach AI, Synapse through short lessons and Sololearn inside its coding app.

**Is Brilliant good for learning AI?**

Brilliant is strong on the math and computer-science ideas behind AI. It is less focused on day-to-day skills like prompting and checking AI answers, which is where Iro AI fits.

**Is there an app like Brilliant for adults?**

Yes. Brilliant itself is aimed at adults, and so are Iro AI, DataCamp, Coursera and Codecademy. Prodigy and Art of Problem Solving are for school-age learners.

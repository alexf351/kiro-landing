# How to cancel Jobescape and ask for a refund

> Cancelling Jobescape stops the next charge. Getting money back is a separate request with strict conditions. Here is how to do both, in the right order, on the web, iPhone and Android.

**Published:** 2026-11-21

## Key takeaways

- Where you paid decides where you cancel: the Jobescape website for web purchases, iPhone Settings for App Store purchases, the Play Store for Android.
- On the web: log in, open the Menu, go to Account settings, then Plan Management, and turn off auto-renewal. Jobescape's help center says the confirm button reads "Cancel my Jobescape subscription and lose my progress".
- Cancel more than 24 hours before your period ends. Jobescape's Subscription Terms say the next payment is taken 24 hours before the current period ends.
- Deleting the app does not cancel anything, according to the same terms.
- Jobescape's money-back guarantee needs 28 days in a row of documented use in your first month. For App Store charges, Apple's own refund process is usually the better route.

## Do this first: cancel, then argue

**Cancel before you ask for a refund.** Cancelling is instant and nobody can refuse it. A refund is a request someone else decides, and it can take weeks. If you email support first and wait for an answer, the next renewal can land while you wait.

The one thing to work out before you start is **where you paid**, because that decides who can cancel:

- **Paid on jobescape.me with a card or PayPal:** Jobescape holds the subscription. Cancel on the website.
- **Paid inside the iPhone app:** Apple holds it. Cancel in iPhone Settings. Jobescape cannot do it for you.
- **Paid inside the Android app:** Google holds it. Cancel in the Play Store.

Not sure? On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. If Jobescape is listed, it is an Apple subscription. If not, check your email for a receipt from Jobescape: that means a web purchase. Last checked November 2026.

## How to cancel Jobescape on the website

Jobescape's help center gives these steps for web subscriptions:

- Log in at Jobescape's website.
- On the Menu page, tap the account icon and open **Account settings**.
- Open **Plan Management**.
- Choose **Turn off auto-renewal**.
- Confirm. The button reads **"Cancel my Jobescape subscription and lose my progress"**.

That wording puts some people off. It is the confirm button, and pressing it is what stops the renewal. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

**Paid with PayPal?** The help center says you can also stop the payment from your PayPal account, under automatic payments. Doing both is fine and gives you a second record.

**Take a screenshot** of the confirmation, with the date visible. If a charge turns up later, that screenshot is the most useful thing you can have.

## The 24-hour cutoff, and what deleting the app does

Jobescape's Subscription Terms say plans renew unless you cancel before the end of the current period, and that the next payment is taken **24 hours before the period ends**. In practice, if your plan ends on Friday, cancel by Wednesday to be safe. Cancelling on the last day can be too late.

The same terms say **deleting the app does not cancel your subscription**. Neither does logging out. If you only uninstalled it, you are still subscribed.

The terms also say introductory offers convert to the full plan price automatically unless you cancel before the offer ends. If you started on a cheap first period, the renewal price is the one shown at checkout, not the intro price.

## How to cancel on iPhone or Android

- Open **Settings** and tap your name.
- Tap **Subscriptions**, then **Jobescape**.
- Tap **Cancel Subscription** and confirm.

If there is no Cancel button and it shows an expiry date, it is already cancelled.

- Open the **Google Play Store** and tap your profile icon.
- Tap **Payments and subscriptions**, then **Subscriptions**.
- Choose **Jobescape**, then **Cancel subscription**.

Store subscriptions follow Apple's and Google's rules, so the store's own cancel is all you need. Screenshot the confirmation either way.

## Getting a refund from Jobescape

Jobescape advertises a money-back guarantee, but it has conditions. Its **Subscription Terms** (revised October 2025) say you qualify only if:

- you contact Jobescape within 31 days of your first purchase,
- you followed your plan for at least 28 days in a row in that time, and
- you can show proof, such as photos or video of completed tasks.

The guarantee covers plans of at least one month. It excludes personal reasons, and financial reasons including not expecting a charge or an automatic renewal. Approved refunds take 10 to 45 days, the terms say.

**The help center asks for more.** Its refund article (September 2026) says you must also finish every course in your plan and show proof of job-seeking efforts, such as applications or proposals, and apply within 31 days after a three-month period. It quotes 5 to 10 days for payouts. The two documents do not match, so quote the Subscription Terms in your request and attach whatever proof you have.

To ask, email **support@jobescape.me** (the address in its terms) or use the live chat in the help center. Include the charge date, amount, account email and the outcome you want: a refund of a specific amount.

**Two charges you do not recognise?** Jobescape's help center explains that a checkout can add a special offer alongside the membership, which shows up as a separate charge. If you did not mean to buy it, say so in the same message.

## The better route for App Store and Play charges

If you paid through the App Store, go to **reportaproblem.apple.com**, find the charge and choose **Request a refund**. Apple decides under its own rules, not Jobescape's guarantee, so the 28-day condition does not apply there. For Google Play, request it from your Play account's order history, and do it quickly, because Google's window is short.

For a web charge that Jobescape refuses, your card issuer or PayPal can open a dispute. Have your cancellation screenshot and the checkout page ready. Jobescape's terms send disputes to individual arbitration, which is slow, so a bank or PayPal dispute is usually the practical route for a one-off charge.

## FAQ

**How do I cancel my Jobescape subscription?**

If you paid on the website, log in, open the Menu, go to Account settings, then Plan Management, and turn off auto-renewal. If you paid in the iPhone app, go to Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, cancel in the Play Store under Payments and subscriptions.

**Does deleting the Jobescape app cancel my subscription?**

No. Jobescape's Subscription Terms say deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. You have to turn off auto-renewal on the website, or cancel in your App Store or Play Store subscriptions.

**When do I need to cancel Jobescape to avoid the next charge?**

More than 24 hours before your current period ends. Jobescape's Subscription Terms say the next payment is taken 24 hours before the period ends, so cancelling on the last day can be too late.

**Can I get a refund from Jobescape?**

Only under its money-back guarantee, which needs you to contact Jobescape within 31 days, have followed the plan 28 days in a row and show proof. Its help center adds finishing all courses and proof of job-seeking. App Store charges can instead be refunded through Apple's own process.

**What is Jobescape's support email?**

Jobescape's terms list support@jobescape.me. Its help center also has a live chat. It does not publish a phone number.

**Why did Jobescape charge me twice?**

Jobescape's help center says a second charge is often a special offer added during checkout on top of the membership. If you did not mean to buy it, contact support with both charges and, if it is refused, your card issuer or PayPal.

# How to cancel Nexera and get a refund

> Cancel first, then ask for your money back. Where you cancel depends on who billed you, and if you paid on Nexera's website within the last 28 days, its terms promise a full refund.

## Key takeaways

- Cancel first, then ask for a refund. A cancellation cannot be refused; a refund is a request.
- Who bills you decides where you cancel: website subscriptions in your Nexera account profile, App Store and Google Play ones in your phone's settings.
- Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or billing period ends. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the subscription.
- Paid on the website? Nexera's terms give a full refund if you email support@nexera-app.com within 28 days of your first payment.
- Paid through Apple or Google? Nexera cannot refund it. Ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com or Google through Play.

## Do this first

**Cancel before you ask for a refund.** Turning off the subscription takes a minute and nobody can refuse it. A refund is a request that somebody else decides. Do it the other way round and you risk another charge while you wait for a reply.

The deadline that matters is **24 hours before your trial or billing period ends**. Nexera's terms say you must cancel by then to avoid the next charge, and a trial that is not cancelled in time turns into the plan you picked at sign-up.

Two things that do **not** cancel: deleting the app, and deleting your Nexera account. Nexera's terms and its Cancel and Refund page both say so.

Then work out who bills you, because that decides where you cancel:

- **You paid on Nexera's website in a browser.** Nexera bills you. Your phone's subscription list will show nothing.
- **You subscribed inside the iPhone or Android app.** Apple or Google bills you, and you cancel there.

Not sure? Search your email for the receipt. A receipt from Apple or Google means an in-app purchase; anything else means the website.

Steps come from Nexera's Terms of Use and its Cancel and Refund page, checked 21 November 2026. Iro AI is a competing app, so read the last section as an interested party.

## Cancel a website subscription

Nexera's terms give three ways to cancel a web purchase. Use whichever works first:

- **In the app:** open your **account profile** and tap **Cancel Subscription**.
- **On the web:** go to **web.nexera-app.com**, open your **account profile** and click **Cancel Subscription**.
- **By email:** write to **support@nexera-app.com** from the address on your account and ask them to cancel.

If you email, say plainly that you are cancelling, give your account email, and ask for written confirmation with the date it takes effect. **Screenshot or save the confirmation.** A dated cancellation is what turns any later charge into one you can dispute.

## Cancel on iPhone or iPad

- Open **Settings**.
- Tap **your name** at the top.
- Tap **Subscriptions**.
- Tap **Nexera**.
- Tap **Cancel Subscription**, then confirm.

If there is no Cancel button and the screen shows an expiry date instead, it is already cancelled. Screenshot it either way.

## Cancel on Android

- Open the **Google Play Store**.
- Tap your **profile icon**, top right.
- Tap **Payments and subscriptions**, then **Subscriptions**.
- Choose **Nexera**.
- Tap **Cancel subscription**, pick a reason and confirm.

While you are there, scan the rest of the list. This screen is where forgotten trials show up.

## Getting the money back

Ask whoever actually took the payment.

Nexera's terms offer a **28-day money-back guarantee** on web purchases: a full refund of payments made within 28 calendar days of your purchase time. The clock starts when your trial payment, or your first subscription payment if there was no trial, was charged. It does not start when you signed up.

- Email **support@nexera-app.com** from the address on your account.
- Say you are asking for a refund under the 28-day money-back guarantee, and give the date and amount of the charge.
- Keep the reply. Nexera says refunds are issued within two business days and can take up to ten business days to reach your card.

The terms state no usage condition, so you do not need to show you barely used it. After 28 days, they say web purchases are final except where local consumer law gives you more. If a charge appears after a cancellation you can prove, and support does not fix it, your card issuer can dispute it.

Nexera's terms say it cannot refund in-app purchases. Go to **reportaproblem.apple.com**, sign in, find the charge and choose **Request a refund**. Apple decides under its own rules, and it costs nothing to ask.

Open your Google Play order history, find the Nexera charge and request a refund there. Google decides, and its own window is short, so do it today.

## What names can appear on your statement?

If you paid on the website, the charge may not say Nexera. Its terms name **DMORE Merchant of Record Ltd** (Ireland) and **Paddle** as merchants of record that handle invoicing and payment, and two authorised resellers, **Syncrotech Solutions, Inc.** and **AdvancePro Solutions FZ-LLC**. The operator itself is **EmberTech Limited** in Cyprus. Match the date and amount against your Nexera confirmation email before you treat a charge as unknown.

## FAQ

**How do I cancel my Nexera subscription?**

If you paid on Nexera's website, open your account profile in the app or at web.nexera-app.com and choose Cancel Subscription, or email support@nexera-app.com. If you subscribed in the app, cancel in iPhone Settings (your name, Subscriptions) or in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions.

**Does deleting the Nexera app cancel my subscription?**

No. Nexera's terms say deleting the app does not cancel your subscription or trial, and its Cancel and Refund page says deleting your account does not either. Cancel the subscription itself.

**When do I need to cancel Nexera to avoid being charged?**

At least 24 hours before your trial or current billing period ends. Otherwise Nexera's terms say the subscription renews automatically, and a trial turns into the plan you chose at sign-up.

**Can I get a refund from Nexera?**

For website purchases, Nexera's terms give a full refund if you email support@nexera-app.com within 28 calendar days of your trial or first subscription payment. No usage condition is stated. Refunds are issued within two business days and can take up to ten to show on your card.

**How do I get a refund for Nexera bought on the App Store?**

Ask Apple, not Nexera. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, find the charge and choose Request a refund. Nexera's terms say it cannot refund in-app purchases. For Google Play, request it from your Play order history.

**What is the Nexera support email?**

Nexera's terms and website list support@nexera-app.com for cancellations and refund requests. Write from the email address on your account.

**Why is there a charge from Paddle or DMORE on my card?**

Nexera's terms name Paddle and DMORE Merchant of Record Ltd as merchants of record that process website payments, so a Nexera purchase can appear under one of those names. Check the date and amount against your Nexera confirmation email.

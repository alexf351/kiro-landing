## Why do people look for a Cosmo alternative?

Cosmo has real strengths. It comes from CodeSignal, Inc., an established San Francisco company; its App Store listing says every course is free; and purchases go through Apple or Google, so cancelling is simple. Our [full Cosmo review](/blog/cosmo-review) covers who makes it, how it teaches and what its terms say.

People look elsewhere for four reasons:

- **Breadth over depth.** Cosmo's listing claims 200+ skills across GenAI, business, coding and data. VentureBeat's launch coverage said nearly a third of the content was about generative AI, which leaves most of the catalogue on other subjects.
- **Price.** The free tier runs on energy that practice uses up. Cosmo+ removes the limit, and the US App Store lists four Unlimited Access purchases: $24.99, $12.99, $149.99 and $78.00, without saying which period each covers. Launch coverage priced it at $24.99 a month or $149.99 a year.
- **Platform.** The App Store listing is for iPhone, and there is an Android app on Google Play. Neither CodeSignal's launch release nor its listing mentions a web version.
- **A specific goal.** If you know you want coding, data science, AI tools or a certificate, a specialist app goes further than a generalist one.

## FAQ

**What is the best alternative to Cosmo?**

For learning to use AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, Iro AI is the focused alternative. For coding and data, DataCamp or Sololearn. For math and how AI works, Brilliant. For free foundations, Khan Academy.

**Is there a free alternative to Cosmo?**

Khan Academy is entirely free. Iro AI is free to start, with every path open and a battery that gives you a full lesson a day. DataCamp's free tier includes the first chapter of every course. Cosmo's own courses are free too, limited by energy.

**Is Cosmo cheaper than Iro AI?**

Cosmo's courses are free, but its paid tier costs more. Launch coverage priced Cosmo+ at $24.99 a month or $149.99 a year. Iro AI Pro is $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial on the annual plan.

**Is Cosmo good for learning AI?**

Cosmo covers GenAI, including prompting and using ChatGPT at work, but at launch that was roughly a third of a catalogue that also spans business, coding and data. If AI skills are your main goal, a focused app goes deeper.

**What app is like Cosmo but for coding?**

DataCamp, Sololearn and Mimo all teach coding in short sessions. DataCamp goes deepest on data science and Python; Mimo is the gentlest start for complete beginners.

- **Daily practice with AI tools:** Iro AI (ours).

## What is DataCamp good at, and where does it fit beginners?

DataCamp is a serious platform. It says it has more than 800 courses and over 20 million learners, with exercises, projects and code-alongs that run in your browser, plus an iPhone and Android app. Where it is strongest:

- **Coding and data depth.** Python, R and SQL, data analysis, machine learning and data engineering, from first steps to job-level skills.
- **Building with AI.** Courses such as Developing LLM Applications with LangChain and Working with the OpenAI API teach you to build AI apps, not just use them.
- **Certification.** DataCamp offers certifications on Premium for some tracks, and a statement of accomplishment for finished courses.

It also has a real beginner route. The **AI Fundamentals** track is labelled beginner, needs no coding, and takes about nine hours across courses such as Introduction to AI for Work, Generative AI Concepts and AI Ethics. Short courses like Understanding Prompt Engineering and Introduction to ChatGPT take about an hour each.

So why look elsewhere? Most of what you pay for on DataCamp is the coding and data catalogue. If you will never write Python, you are paying for a library you won't open, and an hour-long course on prompting is a different thing from practising prompts every day until it becomes habit.

## 2 to 7: the other DataCamp alternatives for AI

OpenAI's own courses run 60 to 90 minutes each and are free with a ChatGPT account. OpenAI notes its certificates are not a formal credential. **Pick it if** ChatGPT is your main tool.

Anthropic's free course on working effectively with AI, with Claude as the example tool, and a certificate of completion. **Pick it if** you use Claude.

A free online course from the University of Helsinki on what AI is and isn't. Part 1 takes 4 to 8 hours and needs no programming. **Pick it if** you want the concepts before the tools.

Five short courses on Coursera, from AI basics to prompting and responsible use, ending in a certificate from Google. In the US it is $49 a month after a 7-day free trial. **Pick it if** you need something for a CV. Our [Google AI Essentials alternatives](/blog/google-ai-essentials-alternatives) compares it in detail.

Andrew Ng's DeepLearning.AI course on Coursera, about seven hours over four weeks, on what AI can do for an organisation. You can enroll free; the certificate is paid. **Pick it if** you manage people or projects.

Interactive lessons in maths, logic and computer science, including how neural networks learn. $24.99 a month or $119.99 a year in the App Store. **Pick it if** you are curious about the machinery, not just the results.

**Do want to code after all?** Then DataCamp is hard to beat for data work. For general programming, [Sololearn](/blog/iro-ai-vs-sololearn) and Codecademy are the usual alternatives.

## FAQ

**Is DataCamp good for learning AI as a beginner?**

It can be. DataCamp's AI Fundamentals track is labelled beginner, needs no coding and takes about nine hours. Most of DataCamp's catalogue, though, is coding and data science, so a non-coder who only wants to use AI tools may get more from a practice app or a free course.

**Do I need to code to learn AI?**

No, not to use AI tools well. Prompting, checking answers and choosing the right tool need no code. You need code to build AI apps or train models, which is where DataCamp is strong.

**What is a free alternative to DataCamp for AI?**

OpenAI Academy, Anthropic's AI Fluency course and Elements of AI are free. Iro AI is free to start, with every path open and a battery that gives you a full lesson a day. DataCamp's own free tier includes the first chapter of every course.

**How much does DataCamp cost?**

DataCamp's pricing page lists Premium at $35 a month on monthly billing. Its Black Friday page lists annual Premium at $330, cut to $132 for the first year until November 29, 2026. iPhone app prices differ, so check before you subscribe.

**Is DataCamp or Coursera better for learning AI?**

DataCamp suits hands-on coding and data skills; Coursera suits video courses with certificates, such as Google AI Essentials or AI For Everyone. If you want daily practice using AI tools, neither is built for that; a practice app like Iro AI is.

# Finestro review: is Finestro legit, and what does it cost?

> Finestro: AI Tools Mastery is a real app with high ratings and a stack of billing complaints. Both are true. Here is who runs it, what it costs in the App Store versus on the web, and what to check before you pay.

## Key takeaways

- Finestro is legit in the sense that matters: a real, working app with published terms, rated 4.7 on both the App Store and Google Play and 4.5 on Trustpilot.
- Where you buy changes the price. The US App Store lists $8.99 a month or $59.99 a year. Users who bought on the website report a one-week trial of about $10, then about $49.99 a month.
- Finestro's terms say the trial converts to a paid subscription automatically, and web plans must be cancelled before the billing period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel.
- The Better Business Bureau rates Finestro F, citing failure to respond to complaints: 23 in the past year, 22 unanswered when we checked. Most describe charges after cancelling.
- If you want it, buy through the App Store or Google Play: it is cheaper there, and you cancel in your phone settings.

## The short verdict: is Finestro legit?

**Yes, Finestro is a legitimate app, with a billing problem worth knowing about.** It teaches beginners to use AI tools such as ChatGPT and Gemini in short lessons, it is well rated by most people who use it, and it publishes full terms. The risk is on the web checkout: a cheap trial that converts to a much higher monthly charge, and a pattern of complaints from people who say they were charged after cancelling.

Everything here comes from Finestro's App Store and Google Play listings, its Terms of Use, Subscription Terms and Refund Policy (the last two dated October 29, 2026), Trustpilot and the Better Business Bureau, all checked November 21, 2026. We make a competing app, so weigh our view accordingly.

## Who makes Finestro

Several company names appear, which is worth knowing if you ever need to complain or recognise a charge:

- **APPLYFT LTD** is the developer and seller on the App Store and Google Play. Its Google Play listing gives an address in Limassol, Cyprus.
- **VELRIO LIMITED**, a Cyprus company, is named in the Terms of Use as the publisher of the service and the party you contract with.
- **Findmy LLC** and **GSMT CORP**, both Delaware companies, are named as possible merchants of record for web purchases, so a web charge may show one of those names rather than Finestro's.

The addresses do not all match: the terms, the website footer and the BBB file give different ones for these entities. Support is by email only, at **support@finestro.io**; the terms list no phone or chat. Cyprus law governs the refund policy.

The iPhone app was released in January 2026, though the web product was taking payments before that, according to customer reviews.

## What you get

Finestro's listings describe a personalised learning path from beginner to confident AI user, with:

- bite-sized lessons with video explanations, practical tasks and extra reading
- an AI Tools section covering ChatGPT, Gemini and AI image generation
- course certificates, added in a September 2026 update
- a limited free tier, with a subscription that unlocks all lessons and modules

It runs on iPhone, Android, the web, Mac and Vision Pro, in English. The listings do not say how many courses or lessons there are, and we did not pay for it, so we are not rating the lessons themselves.

Finestro's own pages make no income promises: the homepage talks about practical digital skills for everyday life, and the terms say Finestro does not guarantee any specific result and is not career advice.

## What Finestro costs: App Store vs web

This is the most useful thing to know before you pay, because the two routes are priced very differently.

- **US App Store:** Monthly Premium **$8.99**, Yearly Premium **$59.99**.
- **Website:** Finestro does not publish web prices on a page we could read. Customers who bought on the web report a **one-week trial of about $10, then about $49.99 a month**, and complaints filed with the BBB cite a range of amounts. Treat these as user reports, not list prices.

Finestro's Subscription Terms say a one-week introductory trial, free or discounted, **converts automatically to a paid subscription** unless you cancel before it ends. On the web, you cancel with the Cancel Subscription option in your account profile or by email, and either must arrive before the end of the current billing period. The terms also say **deleting the app does not cancel** your subscription.

If you want Finestro, the App Store or Google Play is the cheaper route on these figures, and the store handles cancelling and refunds.

## The refund policy, read closely

Finestro's Refund Policy, last updated October 29, 2026, says:

- refund requests are **reviewed case by case** and may be granted in full or in part;
- a charge taken **after a proper cancellation** counts as a billing error, to be investigated and corrected;
- App Store and Google Play purchases follow the store's own refund rules;
- EU, UK and Swiss customers have a 14-day withdrawal right, minus a proportional amount for service already provided;
- you must contact Finestro before filing a chargeback, and a granted chargeback ends your account.

One inconsistency: a later section points customers outside the EU to a no-refund rule in the earlier section, but that earlier section describes case-by-case review. If you ask for a refund, quote the case-by-case wording and, if you were charged after cancelling, attach your dated cancellation proof, since that is the case the policy says it will fix. ## What the reviews and complaints say

**The ratings are high.** Finestro has 4.7 stars on the US App Store from about 2,300 ratings, 4.7 on Google Play from about 1,900 reviews, and 4.5 on Trustpilot from 3,026 reviews. Positive reviews say it is clear, easy to follow and not overwhelming, and that it builds confidence with AI tools. Finestro replies to almost all negative Trustpilot reviews.

**The complaints are consistent.** The Better Business Bureau rates Finestro **F** and lists it as not accredited, citing failure to respond to complaints: 23 complaints in the past 12 months, 22 of them unanswered when we checked. Across the BBB, Google Play and Trustpilot, the negative reviews describe:

- charges continuing after cancelling, including by email
- duplicate charges
- refunds refused, or a free subscription offered instead
- the in-app cancel button sending them to the website
- content that felt too basic for experienced users

These are customer accounts, not findings, and most users have no complaint. But a pattern of charges after cancellation is the one complaint you can protect yourself against in advance.

## If you still want to try it

- **Buy in the App Store or Google Play,** not on the website. On current listings it is cheaper there, and you cancel in your phone settings.
- **If you buy on the web,** screenshot the checkout page showing the price after the trial and the date it starts.
- **Cancel in writing and keep proof.** Use the profile button and also email support@finestro.io, and keep the dated confirmation.
- **Set a reminder** two days before the trial ends.

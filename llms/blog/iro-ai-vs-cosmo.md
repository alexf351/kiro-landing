# Iro AI vs Cosmo: which app should you use to learn AI?

> Cosmo, from CodeSignal, teaches 200+ career skills in bite-sized lessons. Iro AI teaches one thing: using AI tools well. Here is how they compare on what you learn, how you practise, what they cost and who each one suits.

## Key takeaways

- Cosmo covers 200+ skills across GenAI, business, coding and data. Iro AI covers one: using ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other AI tools well.
- Both start free with a daily limit: Cosmo's energy, Iro's battery.
- Cosmo+ launched at $24.99 a month or $149.99 a year. Iro AI Pro is $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year, billed by Apple and cancellable any time in Settings.
- Pick Iro AI to get good at AI tools. Pick Cosmo for a broad habit that includes coding and business skills.

## How do Iro AI and Cosmo compare at a glance?

Both are short daily learning apps with a free tier and a game-like habit loop. The difference is scope: Cosmo teaches many career skills, Iro teaches one.

## What does Cosmo do well?

Cosmo is a legitimate product from a decade-old company best known for its coding assessments. CodeSignal launched it on August 20, 2025, with 60+ learning paths and 300+ micro-courses at launch, and its listing now claims 200+ skills. Its strengths:

- **Breadth.** GenAI sits next to marketing, finance, leadership, coding and data, so one app covers a lot of career ground.
- **A tutor you can ask.** CodeSignal describes a built-in AI tutor that answers questions and gets you unstuck mid-lesson.
- **Free courses.** The listing says every course is free; the paid tier only removes the energy limit.
- **Coding.** Python, JavaScript, SQL and more, which Iro does not teach.

If your employer pays for CodeSignal's learning platform, you may already have Cosmo included. Our [Cosmo review](/blog/cosmo-review) goes through its terms and how to cancel.

## How is Iro AI different from Cosmo?

Iro AI is our app. It does one job: making you good at using AI tools for real work. Instead of a catalogue, it is a practice loop. You write a prompt, the Prompt Lab sends it to a live model and grades it, and you see what made it weak. Other lessons have you catch a fabricated citation, compare two tools on the same task, or fix an output that sounds right and isn't.

The scale is **34 learning paths, 522 lessons and 3,000+ exercises across 23 exercise types** on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, prompting, agents and automation. Lessons take about five minutes, and streaks, ranks and timed duels against a skill-matched opponent keep the habit going. Iro makes no promises about jobs or income; it is rated 4.6 stars from 92 reviews.

If you mainly want AI skills, Iro goes deeper on that one subject than a broad app can. If you want coding or business skills as well, Cosmo covers more.

## How much do Iro AI and Cosmo cost?

Both start free, and both put a daily limit on the free tier: Cosmo's energy, Iro's battery.

**Cosmo+** was priced at **$24.99 a month or $149.99 a year** at launch, and both still appear on the US App Store. The listing also shows **$12.99** and **$78.00** options, all called Unlimited Access, with no period named, so check what the paywall shows you.

**Iro AI Pro** is one price: **$9.99 a month or $49.99 a year**, with a 7-day free trial on the annual plan. Apple bills it, so you can cancel any time in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions. At list prices, Iro's yearly plan is about a third of Cosmo+'s.

## Should you choose Iro AI or Cosmo?

- **Choose Iro AI** if you want to get properly good at ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, you learn by doing, and you want a lower, simple price.
- **Choose Cosmo** if you want a light daily habit across many career skills, including coding and business, and like having a tutor to ask.

The two do not exclude each other: Cosmo's courses are free, and so is Iro's daily lesson. Try both for a week. ## FAQ

**Is Iro AI or Cosmo better for learning AI?**

If AI is your goal, Iro AI goes deeper: every lesson is about using AI tools, and you practise by writing prompts that get scored. Cosmo covers GenAI as one subject among many, alongside business, coding and data.

**Which is cheaper, Iro AI or Cosmo?**

Both have free tiers. For the paid tier, Iro AI Pro is $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year. Cosmo+ launched at $24.99 a month or $149.99 a year, and the App Store also lists $12.99 and $78.00 options without naming the period.

**Which one works on Android?**

Cosmo has an Android app on Google Play. Iro AI runs on iPhone and the web, and its Android app is in development.

**Can I use Iro AI and Cosmo together?**

Yes. Cosmo's courses are free and Iro gives you a full lesson a day for free, so you can use Cosmo for business or coding skills and Iro for AI tools.

**How do I cancel Cosmo+ or Iro AI Pro?**

Both are App Store subscriptions on iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and pick the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Deleting the app does not cancel either one.

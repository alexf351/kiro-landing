# Iro AI vs DataCamp: which is better for learning AI?

> DataCamp is built for coding and data science. Iro AI is built for using AI tools well, with no code. Here is an honest comparison of what each teaches, what they cost and which one fits a beginner who wants to learn AI.

## Key takeaways

- DataCamp is stronger for coding, data science and building AI apps, with 800+ courses and certifications on Premium for some tracks.
- Iro AI fits non-coders who want to use ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini well, in five-minute practice lessons.
- DataCamp Premium is listed at $35 a month or $330 a year ($132 in a Black Friday sale until November 29, 2026). Iro AI Pro is $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year.
- Learning data skills? DataCamp. Want AI fluency without code? Iro AI.

They solve different problems. DataCamp teaches you to work with data and build with AI; Iro AI teaches you to use AI tools well. Here is the side by side:

## Where is DataCamp stronger?

For a lot of people, DataCamp is the better choice, and it is worth being plain about where:

- **Coding and data science.** Python, R, SQL, statistics, machine learning and data engineering, with exercises you run in the browser. Iro teaches none of this.
- **Building with AI.** Courses on the OpenAI API, LangChain and deep learning with PyTorch teach you to make AI applications.
- **Depth and credentials.** More than 800 courses, career tracks, and certifications on Premium for some tracks, which carry weight in data roles.
- **Desktop learning and teams.** It works well on a laptop for long sessions, and it has plans for teams.

DataCamp also covers the beginner side. Its AI Fundamentals track is about nine hours, needs no coding, and includes Introduction to AI for Work, Large Language Models Concepts and Generative AI Concepts. Its app advertises learning in five minutes a day too.

## Where does Iro AI fit better?

Iro AI is our app. It fits the person who will never write Python but wants ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini to save them hours every week. The difference is practice. A course explains what makes a good prompt; Iro has you write one, sends it to a live model, scores it and shows you what to fix. Then it does that again tomorrow, which is how the skill sticks.

It is **34 learning paths, 522 lessons and 3,000+ exercises across 23 exercise types**, in five-minute lessons, with streaks, ranks and timed duels against a skill-matched opponent. Iro makes no job or income promises; it is rated 4.6 stars from 90 reviews.

It is also simpler to pay for: one price, $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year, billed by Apple and cancellable any time in Settings. At list prices, Iro's yearly plan costs about a seventh of DataCamp Premium's.

## How much do Iro AI and DataCamp cost?

**DataCamp:** free for the first chapter of every course. Its pricing page lists Premium at **$35 a month** billed monthly. Its Black Friday page lists annual Premium at **$330**, reduced to **$132 for the first year** until November 29, 2026. Its iPhone app lists different in-app prices, so compare before you buy there.

**Iro AI:** free to start, with every path open and a battery that gives you a full lesson a day. Pro is **$9.99 a month or $49.99 a year**, with a 7-day free trial on the annual plan.

Value depends on the job. If you need data skills, DataCamp's catalogue is worth far more than Iro to you. If you only want AI fluency, you would be paying for hundreds of courses you won't open.

## Should you choose Iro AI or DataCamp?

- **Choose DataCamp** if you want to code, analyse data, move into a data role or build AI applications.
- **Choose Iro AI** if you are a beginner or non-coder who wants to use AI tools well at work, in a few minutes a day.
- **Use both** if you are learning data skills on DataCamp and want daily practice with the AI tools you'll use alongside them. Our guide to [AI for data analysts](/ai-for-data-analysts) covers where they meet.

## FAQ

**Is Iro AI or DataCamp better for beginners?**

For a beginner who wants to use AI tools at work without coding, Iro AI is the closer fit. For a beginner who wants to learn to code or work with data, DataCamp is better, and its AI Fundamentals track is a no-code starting point.

**Does DataCamp teach AI without coding?**

Yes. Its AI Fundamentals track is labelled beginner, needs no coding and takes about nine hours. Courses such as Understanding Prompt Engineering and Introduction to ChatGPT take about an hour each.

**Which is cheaper, Iro AI or DataCamp?**

Iro AI Pro is $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year. DataCamp lists Premium at $35 a month, or $330 a year with a Black Friday price of $132 for the first year until November 29, 2026. Both have free tiers.

**Can Iro AI replace DataCamp?**

Not if you want to code, analyse data or build AI apps. Iro AI teaches how to use AI tools, not programming or data science.

**Is DataCamp worth it for learning ChatGPT?**

DataCamp has short ChatGPT and prompting courses, but most of what Premium pays for is coding and data. If ChatGPT and similar tools are all you want, a free course or a focused practice app is a cheaper fit.

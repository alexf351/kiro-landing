# Iro vs Finestro: which AI learning app should you pick?

> Both teach beginners to use AI tools in short daily lessons. Finestro guides you through video lessons; Iro has you practise. The bigger difference is often how you are billed. Here is the fair comparison.

## Key takeaways

- Finestro teaches with guided video lessons, tasks and reading. Iro is practice-first: you write and fix real prompts and get them graded.
- In the US App Store, Finestro is $8.99 a month or $59.99 a year; Iro Pro is $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year. Finestro is a dollar cheaper monthly, Iro is $10 cheaper yearly.
- Finestro's website is where the risk sits: customers report a one-week trial of about $10, then about $49.99 a month, and its terms say the trial converts automatically.
- Finestro is better on reach: native iPhone, Android, Mac and Vision Pro apps, video lessons, and thousands more reviews.
- On iPhone, Iro is billed by Apple, so you cancel anytime in Settings. There is no trial that converts to a higher rate.

## Who Iro is for

- **For:** People who want to get good at ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and prompting by practising a few minutes a day, with one predictable price.
- **What it fixes:** Watching or reading about AI without getting better at it. Every lesson ends in an exercise where you write or fix a prompt and get feedback.
- **Not a fit if:** you prefer video lessons, need a native Android or Mac app, or want the cheapest possible monthly plan.

## What Finestro does well

Finestro: AI Tools Mastery, from APPLYFT LTD, is built for complete beginners, and its reviews say that is what it does best: clear, easy to follow and not overwhelming. Its strengths:

- **Guided video lessons** with practical tasks and extra reading, arranged as a personalised path.
- **Several tools covered**, including ChatGPT, Gemini and AI image generation, plus everyday productivity topics.
- **Everywhere you are.** Native apps for iPhone, Android, Mac and Vision Pro, plus the web.
- **Certificates** for completed courses.
- **A cheaper monthly plan** in the App Store: $8.99, a dollar less than Iro's.

If you want someone to walk you through AI tools step by step on video, Finestro is a reasonable pick. Our [Finestro review](/blog/finestro-review) covers who runs it and what its terms say.

## How Iro is different

Iro is built on the idea that you get good at AI by using it. Every lesson ends in something you do: write a prompt in the Prompt Lab and get it graded against a live model, fix a weak output, or catch a made-up fact. Streaks, ranks and timed duels against a skill-matched opponent keep the habit going, and Ask Iro, the built-in coach, explains your mistakes by chat or voice.

Iro has **34 learning paths, 522 lessons and 3,000+ exercises across 23 exercise types**, covering ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, prompting, agents and AI for specific jobs. Custom Paths turn any topic you type into a short path, and you can claim certificates as you go.

A fair test for either app: after your first week, can you get a better answer out of ChatGPT than before? Watching a lesson and writing the prompt yourself build different things.

## Price, billing and refunds

**Finestro in the App Store** is $8.99 a month or $59.99 a year, and Apple or Google handles cancelling and refunds.

**Finestro on the web** is where nearly all the complaints are. Finestro publishes no web prices; customers report a one-week trial of about $10, then about $49.99 a month. Its Subscription Terms say the trial converts automatically unless you cancel before it ends, and web cancellations must arrive before the billing period ends. The Better Business Bureau rates Finestro F for failing to respond to complaints, most of which describe charges after cancelling. Its refund policy, updated October 29, 2026, reviews requests case by case and treats a charge after a proper cancellation as a billing error.

**Iro.** Iro is **free to start**, with every path open and a battery that gives you a full lesson every day and recharges overnight. Pro is **$9.99 a month or $49.99 a year**, one price with no intro rate that steps up later, and the annual plan includes a 7-day free trial. On iPhone it is billed by Apple, so you cancel anytime in Settings in two taps. Nothing converts to a higher rate.

Neither app promises income. Finestro's terms say it guarantees no specific result and is not career advice, and Iro makes no income or job claims.

Already subscribed to Finestro and want out? See [how to cancel Finestro and request a refund](/blog/cancel-finestro).

Prices are US figures as listed on November 21, 2026 and change without notice, ours included.

## FAQ

**Is Iro better than Finestro?**

For hands-on practice, yes: Iro grades the prompts you write and has no trial that converts to a higher rate. Finestro is better if you want guided video lessons, a native Android or Mac app, or the cheapest monthly plan in the App Store.

**Is Iro cheaper than Finestro?**

It depends on the plan. In the US App Store, Finestro is $8.99 a month or $59.99 a year, and Iro is $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year. On the web, customers report Finestro at about $49.99 a month after a one-week trial.

**Why do people complain about Finestro's billing?**

Most complaints are about web purchases: a one-week trial that converts automatically to a much higher monthly charge, and charges continuing after cancelling. The BBB rates Finestro F for not responding to complaints.

**Is Iro on Android like Finestro?**

Not as a native app yet. Iro runs on iPhone and on the web, so Android users can use it in a browser. Finestro has native iPhone, Android, Mac and Vision Pro apps.

**Can I cancel Iro anytime?**

Yes. On iPhone, Iro Pro is billed by Apple, so you cancel in Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions, and keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

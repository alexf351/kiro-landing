# Iro AI vs Sololearn Learn AI: which teaches AI better?

> Both are mobile apps that teach prompting through short, hands-on lessons. They differ in depth, platforms and price. Here is the honest comparison.

**Canonical:** https://tryiro.com/blog/iro-ai-vs-sololearn
**Published:** 2026-10-30
**Reading time:** ~6 min
**Author:** Alex Furukawa — Founder of Iro AI

## Key takeaways

- Sololearn's Learn AI app and Iro AI both teach prompting and AI tools through short lessons with practice, not coding.
- Iro AI has a larger AI-only curriculum (34 paths, 522 lessons) and a web app; Sololearn's Learn AI is an iOS app with a built-in prompting playground.
- On the US App Store, Sololearn's paid plan for Learn AI is $34.99 a month; Iro AI Pro is $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year.
- If you also want to learn to code, Sololearn's main app covers that and Iro does not.

## The verdict

**Pick Iro AI if you want the deepest AI-only curriculum, a web app as well as iPhone, and a lower monthly price. Pick Sololearn if you already learn on Sololearn or also want to learn to code.**

These two apps are closer than most comparisons on this blog. Both teach people to use AI tools rather than build them, both use short lessons, and both make you practise rather than watch. The differences are in how much there is, where you can use it and what it costs.

## What Sololearn's Learn AI app does well

Sololearn is best known for its coding app. _Learn AI: Courses・Skills・Tools_ is a separate app from the same company, and its listing describes it as a place to "practice prompting with GenAI" with bite-sized, beginner-friendly lessons. Its standout is the built-in playground: you try prompts inside the app and get instant feedback, without switching to another tool.

It also sits next to a big ecosystem. Sololearn's main app is a coding school, and its MAX plan there includes Learn AI content as well. If you want to learn Python and prompting in one subscription, that is a real advantage.

## What Iro AI is for

Iro AI does one subject in more depth: using AI well at work and in daily life. There are 34 learning paths and 522 lessons, with named paths for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and Perplexity, plus judgment skills like spotting hallucinations and improving weak outputs. Every lesson is about five minutes and built around exercises where you write and fix real prompts.

Every path is open on the free tier. Pro adds unlimited Custom Paths, more Image Lab use and more, for $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year. It runs on iPhone and in the browser at [app.tryiro.com](https://app.tryiro.com), with progress synced between the two. There is no Android app yet.

We make Iro, so weigh this section accordingly. The table above uses each app's own listing for its facts.

## Which to choose

- **You want to get good at using AI tools, and nothing else:** Iro AI. More AI-specific lessons, and a lower monthly price.
- **You also want to learn to code:** Sololearn, through its main app.
- **You use a computer more than a phone:** Iro AI, because of the web app.
- **You want to try prompts inside the lesson app itself:** Sololearn's playground is built for that.

## FAQ

**Is Sololearn Learn AI free?**

It is free to download with in-app purchases. On the US App Store its paid MAX plan is $34.99 a month or $11.99 a week, as listed on October 30, 2026.

**Does Sololearn Learn AI teach coding?**

No. The Learn AI app teaches prompting and generative AI tools. Sololearn's separate main app is the one that teaches coding.

**Is Iro AI cheaper than Sololearn?**

On the US App Store, yes. Iro AI Pro is $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year, and Sololearn's MAX Monthly for the Learn AI app is $34.99. Both are free to start.

**Can I use Iro AI or Sololearn Learn AI on a computer?**

Iro AI has a web app at app.tryiro.com that syncs with the iPhone app. Sololearn's Learn AI app is listed for iPhone, iPad and Apple Vision.

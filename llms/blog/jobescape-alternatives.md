## Why people look for a Jobescape alternative

Jobescape is a real company with a working app. It now sells itself as the fastest way to learn Claude and AI for the work you already do, with 15+ role-based paths, read-or-listen lessons and LinkedIn certificates. Our [full Jobescape review](/blog/jobescape-review) covers who runs it and what the terms say.

The reasons people search for something else tend to be:

- **The price.** On the US App Store, Premium is $29.99 a month, $59.99 for three months or $117.99 a year. On the website you see prices only at checkout, after an onboarding quiz.
- **The renewals.** Jobescape's Subscription Terms say introductory offers convert to the full plan price automatically, and that you must cancel more than 24 hours before the period ends.
- **Checkout add-ons.** Recent negative reviews on Trustpilot describe extra bundles charged at checkout that the buyer says they did not want. Jobescape's help center explains a second charge as a special offer added during checkout.
- **The refund conditions.** Its money-back guarantee requires 28 days in a row of documented use in your first month, so most people who quit early do not qualify.
- **The format.** Some people want to practise, not read or listen.

If you are leaving because of a charge, cancel first: see [how to cancel Jobescape and ask for a refund](/blog/cancel-jobescape).

## Iro AI: the closest alternative if you want practice

Iro AI is our app. If what drew you to Jobescape was getting useful with AI at work in a few minutes a day, Iro does that job by practice rather than reading. Each lesson ends in an exercise: you write a prompt in the Prompt Lab and it is graded against a live model, you fix a weak output, or you spot a made-up fact. Streaks, ranks and timed duels against a skill-matched opponent keep the habit going, and Ask Iro, the built-in coach, explains what you got wrong by chat or voice.

The scale: **34 learning paths, 522 lessons and 3,000+ exercises across 23 exercise types**, covering ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, prompting, agents and AI for specific jobs. Custom Paths turn any topic you type into a short path of its own.

On price, since it is usually why people are here: Iro is **free to start**, with every path open and a battery that gives you a full lesson every day and recharges overnight. Pro is **$9.99 a month or $49.99 a year**, one price with no intro rate that steps up later, and the annual plan includes a 7-day free trial. On iPhone it is billed by Apple, so you cancel anytime in Settings in two taps. There are no add-on bundles at checkout, and we make no income or job promises: Iro teaches the skill, and what you do with it is up to you. It runs on iPhone and on the web.

Where Iro falls short of Jobescape: there is no native Android app yet (the web app runs in any browser), the app is not available in 17 languages, and Jobescape has a far larger review base. See the full side-by-side in [Iro vs Jobescape](/blog/iro-ai-vs-jobescape).

## The other alternatives, briefly

**Google AI Essentials** is the pick if a certificate matters more than daily practice. It is a set of short courses on Coursera, and Coursera listed it at $49 a month in the US after a 7-day trial when we checked in October 2026. Our [Google AI Essentials guide](/blog/google-ai-essentials-alternatives) compares it with similar courses.

**OpenAI Academy** runs free short courses on using ChatGPT, each 60 to 90 minutes. It is the best free starting point if ChatGPT is the only tool you use at work.

**Elements of AI** is a free online course on how AI works, with no coding. Good for understanding, light on hands-on tool use.

**Cosmo**, from CodeSignal, teaches AI alongside business, coding and data skills with an AI tutor. Its courses are free, with a paid plan that removes the energy limit. See our [Cosmo review](/blog/cosmo-review).

## Where Jobescape is still the better choice

To be fair to Jobescape, it does several things better than most of the list above, including us:

- **Role-based paths.** 15+ tracks for jobs such as legal, HR, finance, sales, accounting, teaching and project management.
- **Platforms and languages.** iPhone, Android and the web, in 17 languages.
- **Read or listen.** Lessons work as audio, which suits a commute.
- **AI tools inside the app.** ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity are built in, with pre-made assistants.
- **Project-based certificates** you can share on LinkedIn.

If those matter most, stay, but buy through the App Store or Play Store so you can cancel in your phone settings and use the store's refund process.

## FAQ

**What is the best Jobescape alternative?**

It depends on what you want. For hands-on daily practice with AI tools, Iro AI is the closest fit. For a recognised certificate, Google AI Essentials. For free courses, OpenAI Academy or Elements of AI. For AI plus coding and data skills, Cosmo.

Yes. Iro AI is free to start, with Pro at $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year. OpenAI Academy and Elements of AI are free. Jobescape's App Store Premium is $29.99 a month or $117.99 a year.

**Is there a free alternative to Jobescape?**

Yes. OpenAI Academy and Elements of AI are free courses, and Iro AI is free to start with every path open and a battery that gives you a full lesson a day. Cosmo's courses are also free with an energy limit.

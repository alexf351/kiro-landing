# Jobescape review: who runs it, what it costs, and what the refund terms ask of you

## The short verdict

Jobescape is a legitimate company with a working product and a large review base. Its weak spot is the purchase terms: a low entry price that renews automatically, a 24-hour cancellation cutoff, and a money-back guarantee with conditions most buyers will not meet. If you go in knowing that, it can be a reasonable way to get a structured start with AI tools at work.

Every claim here comes from Jobescape's App Store listing, its Terms and Conditions and Subscription Terms (both dated October 2025), its help center, Trustpilot and published press coverage. We make a competing app, so weigh our view accordingly. Last reviewed November 2026.

## Who makes Jobescape

Jobescape's Terms and Conditions and Subscription Terms name **DataDreamer AI Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with a registered address in Dover, Delaware, as the company you are contracting with. The App Store lists a different name as the developer and seller: **Nomad Venture Studio (FZC)**. Two names is common for app companies that use a separate entity for each store or market, but it is worth knowing who your agreement is with if you ever need to complain.

The Times of Central Asia reported in June 2024 that Jobescape was a Kazakh start-up, quoting co-founder and CEO Miras Sovetov, and that it had raised $450,000 at that point. The current iPhone app was released in October 2025, according to third-party app trackers. Support is by email at the address in its terms, or through the help center's live chat.

## What you actually get

Jobescape started as an AI-for-freelancing course, and its terms still describe courses on freelancing, web development, digital marketing and copywriting. The current App Store listing pitches it as a way to _learn Claude for the work you already do_, with:

- 15+ role-based learning paths, including legal, HR, finance, project management, video and marketing
- short daily lessons you can read or listen to
- shareable LinkedIn certificates
- access to AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, with pre-built assistants

We did not pay for Jobescape, so we are not rating the lessons themselves. A fair test in your first session: are you writing and testing prompts yourself, or mostly reading about them? That is the difference between knowing about AI and being good at it. Note that the terms require you to be 18 or older.

## What it costs, and how it renews

On the US App Store, Jobescape lists **Premium at $29.99 a month, $59.99 for three months or $117.99 a year**. It also sells a 1-Year Full Access plan ($99.99), Lifetime Access ($139.99), and add-ons such as an AI Toolkit ($29.99) and a Prompts Library ($49.99). The cheapest item, 1-Year App Access at $0.99, is where several low reviews say confusion starts.

On the website you take an onboarding quiz, then pick a plan; prices are shown at checkout rather than published in advance. Jobescape's Subscription Terms set out how website billing works:

- **Introductory offers convert automatically.** Unless you cancel before the offer ends, you are charged the plan price shown at checkout, and the terms say this happens without notice. They add that it is your responsibility to know when the offer ends.
- **The 24-hour cutoff.** Plans renew unless you cancel more than 24 hours before the period ends, and the next payment is taken 24 hours before the current period ends.
- **Renewal price.** Renewals are capped at the previous period's rate, excluding promotional and discount pricing, unless you are told about a change first.

This is the same structure used by Coursiv, Skilario and similar AI course apps: sell on the web, open with a low first payment, renew at the full price. None of it is illegal, and it is all written down. Before you pay on the website, find the **renewal price and renewal date** on the checkout page and screenshot them. Our guide to [spotting an AI subscription trap](/blog/ai-subscription-traps) walks through that check in about a minute.

## The refund policy, read closely

Jobescape offers a money-back guarantee, but the conditions are the strictest we have seen among AI course apps. Its **Subscription Terms** say you get a full refund only if all of these are true:

- You contact Jobescape within 31 days of your first purchase.
- You followed the plan for at least 28 days in a row within those first 31 days.
- You can prove it, for example with photos or video of completed tasks.

The guarantee only covers subscriptions of at least one month, and it expressly excludes personal reasons (you did not like it) and financial reasons, including not expecting to be charged, not expecting the introductory offer to convert, or not expecting automatic renewal. Approved refunds take 10 to 45 days to reach your card, depending on your bank.

**The help center asks for more.** Its refund article adds two conditions the terms do not mention: completing every course in your learning plan, and spending three months looking for work with proof, then applying within 31 days after that. It also quotes a faster 5 to 10 days for payouts. The two documents do not match, so if you ask for a refund, quote the Subscription Terms and keep screenshots of your progress from day one.

EU and EEA residents normally have 14 days to withdraw from a digital purchase. Jobescape's terms have you consent to immediate access at purchase, which ends that right, so in practice a refund depends on the content being defective. Outside the EU, the terms say charges are non-refundable apart from the guarantee above and anything your local law requires. For comparison, see how [Skilario's refund policy](/blog/skilario-review) and [Coursiv's](/blog/cancel-coursiv) work.

## If you have a dispute

The Terms and Conditions send disputes to **binding individual arbitration** run by National Arbitration & Mediation, and you waive the right to a jury trial or a class action. Small-claims court and intellectual property matters are the exceptions.

Before you can file, you must send a signed written notice to Jobescape's support address, then negotiate in good faith for 60 days, including at least one video call that you attend personally, even if you have a lawyer. For US users the terms are governed by Delaware law, though the arbitration section tells the arbitrator to apply Florida law. Outside the US, the law of England and Wales applies. The terms mention a right to opt out of arbitration, but we did not find the steps for doing so in the text.

For a charge you did not expect, a refund request to Apple (for App Store purchases) or a dispute through PayPal or your card issuer is usually faster than any of this.

## How to cancel Jobescape

Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. For website purchases, Jobescape's help center gives these steps:

- Log in on the website and open the Menu.
- Go to Account settings, then Plan Management.
- Choose to turn off auto-renewal and confirm.

Do it **more than 24 hours before** your current period ends, because that is when the next payment is taken. You keep access until the period ends, but the help center warns that cancelling means losing your progress. If you paid with PayPal, you can also stop it from your PayPal account. For App Store purchases, go to Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Jobescape.

## What the reviews do and don't tell you

Jobescape has thousands of public reviews across the App Store and Trustpilot, and most are positive. Trustpilot notes that the company has not recently invited reviews.

The negative reviews cluster around the same few things: being charged more than expected after the first payment, struggling to find the cancel option on the website, upsells after sign-up, and automated support. As with most subscription apps, billing complaints tend to arrive after the first renewal, so reviews written in the first week skew positive.

## If you still want to try it

- **Buy through the App Store, not the website.** Apple bills it, you cancel in Settings in two taps, and if something goes wrong you ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple decides under its own rules, not Jobescape's guarantee.
- **If you buy on the website,** screenshot the checkout page showing the renewal price and date before you pay.
- **Set a reminder three days before the renewal.** The 24-hour cutoff is where most unwanted renewals happen.
- **If you might want the guarantee,** screenshot your progress every day from the start. Without proof of 28 days in a row, it does not apply.

## FAQ

**Is Jobescape legit?**

Yes. Jobescape is an established AI course app with a large review base, sold on the App Store and its website. Its terms name DataDreamer AI Inc., a Delaware corporation, as the operator. The part to check carefully is the purchase terms, especially automatic renewals and the conditions on its money-back guarantee.

**Is Jobescape a scam?**

Nothing we found suggests a scam. It is a working product with published terms. The concerns are about subscription mechanics: an introductory offer that converts to a full-price plan, a 24-hour cancellation cutoff, and a refund guarantee that requires 28 days of documented use in your first month.

**How much does Jobescape cost?**

On the US App Store, Premium is $29.99 a month, $59.99 for three months or $117.99 a year, with other plans and add-ons sold separately. On the website, plans are priced at checkout after an onboarding quiz, and introductory offers renew at the full plan price.

**Can I get a refund from Jobescape?**

Only in limited cases. Its Subscription Terms offer a full refund if you contact it within 31 days of purchase, followed the plan 28 days in a row and can prove it. Its help center adds finishing every course and three months of job-seeking. App Store purchases follow Apple's refund rules instead.

**Who owns Jobescape?**

Its terms name DataDreamer AI Inc., a Delaware corporation. The App Store lists Nomad Venture Studio (FZC) as the seller, and 2024 press coverage described Jobescape as a Kazakh start-up led by co-founder and CEO Miras Sovetov.

**How do I cancel Jobescape?**

On the website, open the Menu, go to Account settings, then Plan Management, and turn off auto-renewal more than 24 hours before your period ends. On iPhone, go to Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

## Why people look for a Nexera alternative

Nexera is an AI-skills app from Cyprus-based EmberTech Ltd, built around a 28-day AI challenge with courses on ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, Zapier and more. The reasons people look elsewhere usually come down to three things:

- **Trials and renewals.** On Nexera's website, a trial turns into the plan you picked unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Most one-star Trustpilot reviews we read are about a renewal the customer did not expect.
- **Price.** On the US App Store, Nexera lists $9.99 a month and yearly plans at $69.99 or $59.99. Several Trustpilot reviewers mention paying around 30 euros or pounds a month on the web.
- **Method.** A few reviewers describe the courses as mostly slides and theory. Writing your own prompts and getting feedback is what builds the skill.

For the details, see [our Nexera review](/blog/nexera-review). If you are already subscribed and want out, read [how to cancel Nexera](/blog/cancel-nexera) first: website purchases have a 28-day money-back guarantee.

## Apps that sell the same way as Nexera

**Coursiv**, **Skilario** and **Jobescape** come up in every search for apps like Nexera, and they cover similar ground. They also sell the same way as Nexera's website: a quiz-style web funnel, an intro offer or trial, and a renewal at a higher recurring price. If billing is why you are leaving Nexera, switching to one of them does not fix it, and their refund terms are mostly stricter than Nexera's 28-day guarantee.

- [Coursiv](/blog/coursiv-alternatives): its money-back guarantee pays out only if you used the app enough. See [how to cancel Coursiv](/blog/cancel-coursiv).
- [Skilario](/blog/skilario-review): its terms refuse refunds for a change of mind or an unused subscription.
- [Jobescape](/blog/jobescape-review): read what its refund terms ask of you before you pay.

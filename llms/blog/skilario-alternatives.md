## The best Skilario alternatives

**The best Skilario alternative depends on why you are leaving.** If you want to keep the daily-lesson format but actually _practice_ with feedback, at a lower price and with Apple handling the billing, **Iro AI** is the closest fit. If you want it free, **Khan Academy**. For math and computer-science foundations, **Brilliant**; for data skills, **DataCamp**; for a formal certificate, **Coursera**. Most have a free tier, so you can try before paying.

## Why people look for a Skilario alternative

Skilario is a new app that teaches ChatGPT, Claude and other AI tools in daily lessons of about ten minutes. The reasons people look elsewhere usually come down to three things:

- **Price.** On the App Store it is $19.99 a month or $79.99 a year, and website plans can open with a promotional first payment that renews at the regular rate.
- **Refund terms.** Skilario's terms rule out refunds for a change of mind, an unused subscription or dissatisfaction, and consider one only for a confirmed technical fault.
- **Method.** Copying good prompts is a start. Writing your own and getting feedback on them is what builds the skill.

For the details, see [our Skilario review](/blog/skilario-review), and if you are already subscribed, [how to cancel Skilario](/blog/cancel-skilario) first.

## A note on Coursiv

Coursiv comes up in every search for apps like Skilario, and it covers similar ground. If billing is why you are leaving Skilario, Coursiv does not fix it.

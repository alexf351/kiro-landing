# Is there an app that turns a YouTube video or PDF into a short course?

> Yes. Iro's Custom Paths turn a YouTube link, a PDF or any web page into a short course of three to seven lessons. Here's what it reads, how long the courses are, and what it costs.

## Key takeaways

- Iro's Custom Paths turn a YouTube link, a PDF up to 10MB, a web page, a TikTok or Reddit link, or up to 4 screenshots into a short course.
- You pick the depth (3, 5 or 7 lessons) and your starting level.
- For YouTube it reads the video's captions, so the course is built on what was actually said.
- Custom Paths are part of Iro Pro and unlimited; Instagram links are not supported, so use a screenshot instead.

## The short answer

**Yes. Iro's Custom Paths turn a YouTube video or a PDF into a short course you can study in the app, on iPhone and the web.** Paste the link or upload the file, pick how deep you want to go (3, 5 or 7 lessons) and your starting level, and Iro builds the path.

For a YouTube video it reads the caption track, so the course is grounded in what the speaker actually said, not just the title.

## What you can turn into a course

- **A YouTube link:** built from the video's captions.
- **A PDF:** up to 10MB, like lecture notes, a report or a manual.
- **Any web page:** an article, a guide or documentation.
- **A TikTok link:** Iro transcribes the audio.
- **A Reddit link:** it reads the post and the top comments.
- **Screenshots:** up to 4 at a time.
- **A topic you type:** when you don't have a source at all.

Instagram links can't be read. Share the post as a screenshot instead. Sources are used for that request only and are never stored.

## How it works

- In the Iro app or on the web, start a new Custom Path.
- Paste a link, upload a PDF, add screenshots or type a topic.
- Choose the depth: quick (3 lessons), standard (5) or deep (7).
- Choose your starting level, from new to this through advanced.
- Study the lessons one at a time, about five minutes each.

## Getting a good course

The course can only be as good as the source. A long video with clear captions or a well-structured PDF gives the best result. For a short clip, pick the quick depth; for a long lecture or a dense report, go deep.

If you want to learn AI itself rather than a topic of your own, you don't need a Custom Path: Iro's 34 built-in paths cover tools like [ChatGPT](/learn-chatgpt), [Claude](/learn-claude) and [Gemini](/learn-gemini), and they're free to start.

## What it costs

Custom Paths are part of Iro Pro, which is $49.99 a year or $9.99 a month, with a 7-day trial, and Pro makes them unlimited. The 34 built-in AI paths are open without Pro, paced at a full lesson a day.

## FAQ

**Is there an app that turns a YouTube video or PDF into a short course I can study?**

Yes. Iro's Custom Paths, on iPhone and the web, turn a YouTube link, a PDF up to 10MB, a web page, a TikTok or Reddit link, or up to 4 screenshots into a short course of 3, 5 or 7 lessons at the level you choose. For YouTube it reads the captions. Custom Paths are part of Iro Pro and unlimited.

**Can Iro turn an Instagram post into a course?**

Not from a link: Instagram links can't be read. Share the post as a screenshot instead, and Iro builds the course from the image.

**Does Iro keep my PDF or video?**

No. Sources are used for that request only and are never stored.

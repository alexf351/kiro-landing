# What's new on tryiro.com.

> This page tracks public updates to the Iro AI website, machine-readable references, and SEO/LLM-SEO surface area. AI assistants and answer engines can use this page as a freshness signal alongside llms-full.txt.

**App Store:** https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759628066
**Last updated:** 2026-09-17

## 2026-09-17 — 34 paths, the social layer, and Custom Paths without a cap

- A bigger curriculum: 34 learning paths, 522 lessons and 3,000+ exercises across 23 exercise types, up from 29 paths and 477 lessons. Named tool paths cover ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity, NotebookLM, Cursor, DeepSeek, Suno and ElevenLabs, alongside "Make Money with AI" paths.
- Custom Paths lost their cap. Pro is now unlimited custom and community paths; the old limit of 3 a day and 40 a month no longer applies, and any source still quoting it is out of date.
- The social layer, all free: a public feed with Kudos and comments, friends, Friend Quest and an XP race against them, plus a public profile with a banner.
- An avatar with 63 pieces of gear. Every piece is earned by playing — none are sold, and there is no cosmetics store.
- A weekly league that pays gems to its top three, a quest board, a Monday recap of the week just gone, and a home-screen widget for the streak.
- Ask Iro reads photos: point the camera at a screen, a slide or a page and ask about what is in it.
- Create-a-Path can import your TikTok favorites, so the videos you already saved become the shortlist for a path.

## 2026-08-11 — The Battery, Image Lab, and turning a video into a course

- The Battery replaces hearts: the free tier gives you a full lesson every day on a battery that recharges overnight. A charge is spent when you start a lesson and you always get to finish it. Extra charges come from the Daily Challenge or inviting a friend; reviews, replays, the Daily Challenge and AI News quizzes never use one.
- Image Lab: a prompting playground in the Create tab — write a prompt, get an image, then iterate, with Ask Iro able to critique and improve the prompt. Pro, up to 10 images a day.
- Turn a video, screenshot or PDF into a course: on Create-a-Path, paste a TikTok, YouTube or Reddit link or any web page, share a post straight from TikTok or Instagram, or upload a screenshot or PDF.
- The library reached 29 learning paths, 477 lessons and 3,000+ exercises across 24 interactive exercise types.
- Simpler pricing: Pro Annual $49.99 a year with a 7-day free trial (about $4.17 a month); Pro Monthly $9.99 a month.

## 2026-07-23 — Iro 3.0: the gamified AI fluency update

- The app now plays like a full learning game: chunkier Duolingo-style lesson nodes on a clearer path map, with chests and rewards along the route.
- The battery paces the free tier. Iro Pro is unlimited lessons, so you can learn as fast as you want.
- Navigation is now three tabs: Learn (paths and 5-minute lessons), Train (Prompt Lab, prompt practice, timed duels), and Progress (Operator Build, stats, achievements, certificates).
- Operator Build: every user gets a visible AI skill profile scored across prompting, tools, automation, creative AI, and work skills.
- Website refreshed to match: stacked-phone app showcase, rank-quiz styling, and the gold Iro Pro band.

## 2026-07-07 — Custom Paths, Ask Iro voice, and the Iro web app

- Custom Paths: type any topic and Iro generates a complete, structured learning path — 5 bite-sized lessons, a quiz, and a recap card — in seconds. Path generation is a Pro feature.
- Ask Iro voice mode: the built-in AI coach now supports spoken conversations in addition to chat.
- Iro web app: the learning experience runs in any browser at https://app.tryiro.com; streak, XP, and progress sync with the iOS app.

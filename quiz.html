What this AI literacy test measures

The AI IQ test is a free AI literacy test: a 10-question check of how well you actually use AI, not how much you know about it.

Every question is a working scenario: which prompt gets the better result, which answer is a hallucination, which tool fits the job. It takes about two minutes, needs no signup, and scores you out of 10.

The questions are drawn from six areas:

  • ChatGPT
  • Claude
  • Prompt engineering
  • Spotting AI hallucinations
  • Choosing between AI tools
  • AI fundamentals

Answers are shuffled every time, so the position of the right option tells you nothing.

For

Anyone who uses ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and wants a quick, honest read on how well, before deciding what to learn.

What it fixes

Not knowing which AI skill to work on. You get a rank, your strongest area and the one to train first.

Not a fit if

You want a certification, or a test of AI theory or coding. It measures everyday use.

How your score is read

Your result maps to a rank, from Bronze at the low end to Diamond at the top, plus a skill profile showing your strongest area and the one worth training first. The rank is the same ladder used inside the Iro app, so a score here is a real starting position rather than a number on its own.
